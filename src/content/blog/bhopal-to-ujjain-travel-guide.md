# Bhopal to Ujjain Travel Guide – Distance, Route & Best Transport Options

Ujjain is one of the most sacred cities in India and a major pilgrimage destination. Many devotees traveling to Ujjain from **Bhopal**, the capital of Madhya Pradesh, look for convenient and affordable travel options. This guide covers everything you need to know about traveling from Bhopal to Ujjain, including distance, road routes, train and bus options, and tips for a comfortable journey.

## Distance Between Bhopal and Ujjain

The distance between Bhopal and Ujjain is approximately **193 km** by road. The travel time varies depending on the mode of transport and traffic conditions:

| Mode of Transport | Distance | Travel Time |
|-------------------|----------|-------------|
| **Car / Taxi** | ~193 km | 3.5 – 4.5 hours |
| **Bus** | ~193 km | 4 – 5 hours |
| **Train** | ~190 km | 4 – 5 hours |

The route is well-connected and offers a smooth travel experience. The NH47 highway is the main road connecting the two cities.

## Road Route from Bhopal to Ujjain

The most popular road route from Bhopal to Ujjain is via the **NH47 highway**. Here is the step-by-step route:

1. Start from Bhopal city center
2. Take the NH47 towards Dewas
3. Pass through **Dewas** (a major town on the route)
4. Continue on NH47 towards Ujjain
5. Arrive at Ujjain city

The road is well-maintained with good signage. There are several restaurants, dhabas, and fuel stations along the way, making the journey comfortable. Many travelers also stop at Dewas for a quick meal or tea break.

## By Car or Taxi

Traveling by car or taxi is the most convenient option from Bhopal to Ujjain. Here is what you need to know:

### Private Car
If you have your own vehicle, the drive is pleasant and takes about **3.5 to 4.5 hours**. The NH47 highway is four-lane in most sections and offers smooth driving. You will pass through the countryside of Madhya Pradesh, which is beautiful during the winter months.

### Taxi / Cab
If you do not have a private car, you can hire a taxi from Bhopal. Options include:
- **Ola / Uber:** Available in Bhopal; book in advance for a fixed fare
- **Local taxi operators:** Available at the railway station and bus stand; negotiate the fare
- **Outstation cab services:** Several operators offer Bhopal to Ujjain cab services at competitive rates

Taxi fares typically range from **₹2,500 to ₹4,000** for a one-way trip, depending on the type of vehicle. Shared taxis are also available at a lower cost.

## By Bus

Buses are a popular and affordable option for traveling from Bhopal to Ujjain. Here are the details:

### MP Tourism Buses
Madhya Pradesh Tourism operates comfortable AC and non-AC buses from Bhopal to Ujjain. These buses depart from the **Inter-State Bus Terminal (ISBT)** in Bhopal and are a reliable option.

### Private Bus Operators
Several private operators also run buses between Bhopal and Ujjain. Buses are available throughout the day, with the first bus departing around 6:00 AM and the last around 10:00 PM.

### Bus Stops in Ujjain
Buses from Bhopal arrive at the **Ujjain Bus Stand**, which is located near the city center. From here, you can take an auto-rickshaw or local taxi to reach the Mahakaleshwar Temple or your accommodation.

### Bus Fare
- **Non-AC bus:** ₹150 – ₹250 per person
- **AC bus:** ₹300 – ₹500 per person

## By Train

There are several train options between Bhopal and Ujjain. Here are the popular ones:

### Bhopal–Ujjain Passenger
A daily passenger train that stops at all major stations between Bhopal and Ujjain. It is the most affordable option.

### Bhopal–Indore Intercity Express
This express train runs via Ujjain and is a convenient option for travelers heading to Indore or Ujjain.

### Bhopal Junction (BPL) to Ujjain Junction (UJN)
The railway distance between Bhopal Junction and Ujjain Junction is approximately **190 km**. The journey takes about **4 to 5 hours** depending on the train.

### Train Fare
- **Sleeper class:** ₹120 – ₹180 per person
- **AC 3-tier:** ₹300 – ₹400 per person

### From Ujjain Railway Station
Ujjain Junction is well-connected to the city center. Auto-rickshaws and local taxis are available outside the station. The Mahakaleshwar Temple is about 3 km from the station.

## Best Time to Travel from Bhopal to Ujjain

The best time to travel from Bhopal to Ujjain is during the **winter months (October to March)**. The weather is pleasant and the roads are in good condition. Avoid traveling during the summer months (April to June) when temperatures can exceed 40 degrees Celsius, making the journey uncomfortable.

If you are visiting during **Mahashivratri** or **Simhastha**, expect heavy traffic and book your transport well in advance.

## Tips for a Comfortable Journey

Here are some practical tips to make your Bhopal to Ujjain journey more comfortable:

1. **Book in advance:** During festival seasons, bus and train tickets sell out quickly. Book at least a week in advance.
2. **Carry water and snacks:** There are dhabas along the route, but it is always good to carry your own water and light snacks.
3. **Check the weather:** Avoid traveling during heavy rains or foggy conditions.
4. **Start early:** If traveling by car, start early in the morning to avoid traffic and reach Ujjain by afternoon.
5. **Carry ID:** Keep your ID proof handy, especially if you are traveling by train.
6. **Use GPS:** The NH47 is well-marked, but using a GPS navigation app can help you avoid road closures or diversions.

## Key Stops and Attractions Along the Bhopal-Ujjain Route

The NH47 highway from Bhopal to Ujjain passes through several interesting towns and landmarks. Knowing these stops can make your journey more enjoyable and help you plan rest breaks effectively.

### Dewas: The Midway Stop

Dewas is the most significant town on the Bhopal-Ujjain route, located approximately halfway between the two cities. It serves as an ideal midway break for travelers covering the 193 km distance.

- **Distance from Bhopal:** ~85 km
- **Distance from Ujjain:** ~108 km
- **Typical travel time from Bhopal to Dewas:** About 1.5 to 2 hours

Dewas offers several amenities for road travelers:
- **Dhabas and restaurants:** Popular stops like Guru Kripa and Highway Delight serve delicious North Indian and local Madhya Pradesh cuisine. Many travelers stop here for a hot meal or tea.
- **Fuel stations:** Multiple petrol pumps from brands like Indian Oil, HP, and Bharat Petroleum are available at regular intervals.
- **Restrooms and parking:** Clean facilities and safe parking are available at most dhabas and fuel stations.
- **ATMs and pharmacies:** Available at major junctions in Dewas for any last-minute needs.

If you have extra time and want to explore, you can also visit the **Dewas Tekri**, a famous hilltop temple dedicated to Goddess Chamunda. The temple offers panoramic views of the city and is a popular local pilgrimage spot.

### Gautampura and Maksi: Scenic Countryside Stops

Smaller towns like **Gautampura** and **Maksi** also dot the route, offering additional dhabas and rest opportunities. The countryside around these areas is particularly scenic during the monsoon season when the fields are lush green, and during winter when the weather is cool and pleasant.

Many travelers enjoy stopping at these smaller dhabas for a more authentic rural experience. These stops are also great for buying fresh local snacks and chai from family-run establishments.

## Complete Budget Guide for Bhopal to Ujjain Travel

Understanding the full cost of your journey helps you plan better. Here is a detailed budget breakdown for different transport options and travel styles:

### Transport Cost Comparison

| Transport Mode | Budget Range | What You Get |
|----------------|-------------|--------------|
| **MP Tourism Bus (Non-AC)** | ₹150 – ₹250 per person | Basic seating, 4–5 hours |
| **MP Tourism Bus (AC)** | ₹300 – ₹500 per person | Comfortable AC seating, 4–5 hours |
| **Train (Sleeper)** | ₹120 – ₹180 per person | Basic berth, 4–5 hours |
| **Train (AC 3-Tier)** | ₹300 – ₹400 per person | AC berth, 4–5 hours |
| **Shared Taxi** | ₹500 – ₹800 per person | Door-to-door, 3.5–4.5 hours |
| **Private Taxi (one-way)** | ₹2,500 – ₹4,000 | Entire vehicle, flexible timing |
| **Private Car (own vehicle)** | Fuel cost ~₹1,500–₹2,500 | Complete flexibility |

### Additional Expenses to Budget For

- **Local transport in Ujjain:** ₹100–₹500 depending on whether you use auto-rickshaws or private taxis
- **Meals en route:** ₹200–₹800 per person depending on the type of restaurant
- **Prasad and temple offerings:** Variable, budget ₹200–₹500
- **Parking fees:** ₹20–₹50 at most public parking areas near temples

### Money-Saving Strategies

1. **Travel in a group:** Splitting a private taxi cost makes it comparable to bus fares while offering much more comfort.
2. **Choose trains for long journeys:** If you are not in a hurry, trains are the most economical option.
3. **Book MP Tourism buses online:** Advance booking often gets you better seats and sometimes discounted fares.
4. **Avoid peak festival pricing:** Transport and accommodation rates can double during Mahashivratri and Simhastha. Plan ahead.
5. **Use shared auto-rickshaws:** In Ujjain, shared autos are much cheaper than private cabs for short distances.

## Things to Know Before You Go

Before you embark on your journey from Bhopal to Ujjain, review this checklist to ensure a smooth and spiritually fulfilling trip:

### Pre-Journey Checklist

- [ ] **Book transport tickets in advance** during festival seasons (Mahashivratri, Simhastha, Amavasya, etc.)
- [ ] **Verify your vehicle condition** if driving yourself (engine oil, tire pressure, brakes, spare tire, and toolkit)
- [ ] **Carry sufficient drinking water** — especially important during summer months (April to June)
- [ ] **Keep a government-issued ID proof** handy for train travel, hotel check-in, and temple entry requirements
- [ ] **Download offline maps** of the Bhopal-Ujjain route in case of poor mobile network coverage in rural stretches
- [ ] **Carry some cash** — while digital payments are widely accepted in Bhopal and Ujjain, small dhabas and street vendors may prefer cash
- [ ] **Check the weather forecast** before departure, particularly during the monsoon season (July to September)
- [ ] **Pack light snacks** such as energy bars, dry fruits, and fruits for the journey
- [ ] **Charge your phone fully** and carry a portable power bank for navigation and emergency calls
- [ ] **Wear comfortable, breathable clothing** for the journey and modest attire for temple visits

### Road Travel Specific Tips

- [ ] **Stick to NH47** as the primary route — avoid taking alternative rural roads that may be poorly maintained
- [ ] **Dewas is the main midway point** for rest breaks, refueling, and meals
- [ ] **Keep small change handy** for toll plazas and parking fees along the route
- [ ] **Observe speed limits** — speed cameras and police checkpoints are common in certain stretches
- [ ] **Save emergency contact numbers** — keep the helpline numbers for MP Roadways and local police handy
- [ ] **Avoid night travel** during monsoon season when the road can be dark and slippery in sections

### Temple Visit Preparation

If you are visiting the Mahakaleshwar Temple after reaching Ujjain:
- [ ] **Dress modestly** — avoid shorts, sleeveless tops, and revealing clothing; carry a scarf or shawl if needed
- [ ] **Remove footwear** before entering the temple complex; wear easily removable shoes or sandals
- [ ] **Carry minimal belongings** — large bags, cameras, and mobile phones may not be allowed inside the sanctum; check the [Mahakaleshwar mobile phone rules](/blog/mahakaleshwar-mobile-phone-rules/) before visiting
- [ ] **Check darshan timings** before planning your visit — the temple has specific timings for different aartis
- [ ] **Book Bhasma Aarti tickets in advance** if you want to attend this special ceremony; refer to our [Bhasma Aarti booking guide](/blog/how-to-book-bhasma-aarti-mahakaleshwar/) for details
- [ ] **Carry Prasad items** if you wish to offer them at the temple; local shops near the temple sell ready-made prasad packets

## Things to Do in Ujjain After Reaching from Bhopal

Once you reach Ujjain from Bhopal, here are the top things to do:

### Visit Mahakaleshwar Temple
The Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga is the most important temple in Ujjain. Plan your visit early in the morning for a peaceful darshan experience. For detailed information about darshan timings, booking procedures, and tips, refer to the [Mahakaleshwar Darshan Guide](/blog/mahakaleshwar-darshan-guide/).

### Explore Shipra Ghats
Take a walk along the Shipra Ghats and enjoy the serene atmosphere. Many pilgrims take a holy dip in the Shipra River, especially during auspicious occasions.

### Visit Ram Ghat and Harsiddhi Temple
Ram Ghat is a beautiful ghat on the Shipra River, and the Harsiddhi Temple is a famous temple dedicated to Goddess Harsiddhi. Both are easily accessible from the city center.

### Try Local Cuisine
Ujjain is famous for its **poha and jalebi**, which you can enjoy at local shops near the Mahakaleshwar Temple. There are also several good vegetarian restaurants in the city.

## FAQs About Bhopal to Ujjain Travel

**Q: Is the road from Bhopal to Ujjain safe for night travel?**
A: The NH47 is generally safe, but it is better to avoid night travel, especially during the monsoon season, as the road can be dark and foggy in some sections.

**Q: Can I book a cab from Bhopal to Ujjain for a round trip?**
A: Yes, many taxi operators offer round-trip services from Bhopal to Ujjain. A round trip typically costs ₹4,000 to ₹6,000, depending on the vehicle type.

**Q: Are there rest stops on the Bhopal to Ujjain route?**
A: Yes, there are several dhabas and restaurants along the NH47, especially near Dewas. These are good places to stop for a meal or tea break.

**Q: How far is Indore from Ujjain? Can I visit both cities?**
A: Indore is about 55 km from Ujjain. If you are traveling from Bhopal, you can visit both Indore and Ujjain in the same trip. Many travelers spend a day in Indore and then proceed to Ujjain.

**Q: Are there ATMs and fuel stations on the route?**
A: Yes, there are ATMs and fuel stations at regular intervals along the NH47, including at Dewas and other major towns.

**Q: Is it safe to travel to Ujjain from Bhopal by bus?**
A: Yes, buses from Bhopal to Ujjain are generally safe and reliable. MP Tourism buses are the safest option. Private buses are also a good choice if you choose a reputable operator.

**Q: Can I visit Ujjain as a day trip from Bhopal?**
A: Yes, Ujjain can be visited as a day trip from Bhopal. The 4-hour journey each way means you will have about 4-5 hours to explore the city. However, for a more relaxed pilgrimage experience, it is better to stay overnight.

**Q: What should I pack for the Bhopal to Ujjain trip?**
A: Pack comfortable clothing, sturdy walking shoes, a reusable water bottle, basic first-aid supplies, sunscreen, a hat or cap, and any personal medications. If you plan to visit temples, carry modest clothing that covers your shoulders and knees.
