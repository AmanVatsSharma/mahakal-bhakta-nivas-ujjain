If you are flying to Ujjain, your flight will land at **Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport (IDR) in Indore** — the nearest airport to Ujjain, approximately 55 km away. Ujjain itself does not have a commercial airport. This guide covers everything about the airport, flights, and the onward journey to Mahakaleshwar Temple.

## About Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport

**Airport Code:** IDR
**Location:** Indore, Madhya Pradesh (Rau area)
**Distance from Ujjain:** 55-60 km
**Travel time to Ujjain:** 1.5-2 hours by taxi

Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport is one of the busiest airports in Madhya Pradesh and is named after the legendary Holkar queen who was a great patron of temple architecture. Queen Ahilyabai Holkar is credited with renovating and rebuilding numerous temples across India, including several in Ujjain and the famous Kashi Vishwanath Temple in Varanasi. The airport serves as the primary air gateway to the Malwa region, connecting Indore, Ujjain, and surrounding areas to major Indian cities.

### Airport History and Development

The airport was originally built during World War II as a military airfield. It was upgraded to a civil airport in the 1960s and has undergone several expansions since then. The current terminal building was inaugurated in 2018, featuring modern facilities and a capacity to handle over 1 million passengers annually. The airport has been consistently ranked among the best regional airports in India.

## Airport Facilities

Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport has:

- **Departure and arrival terminals** with basic shopping, ATMs, currency exchange, and restaurants
- **Prepaid taxi counter** — available in the arrival hall for onward travel
- **Food stalls** — IRCTC food plaza and local vendors inside the terminal
- **Wi-Fi** — Available in the departure and waiting areas
- **Car rental counters** — Ola, Uber pick-up zones are outside the terminal
- **Parking** — Short-term and long-term parking available
- **Medical facilities** — Basic first aid at the airport
- **Prayer room** — A small prayer room is available for passengers

The airport is compact and easy to navigate — you will not spend more than 15-20 minutes collecting luggage and exiting.

### Airport Tips

- Arrive at the airport at least 1.5 hours before domestic flights (2 hours during peak season)
- Keep your ID handy for security checks
- The airport has limited dining options inside — eat before arriving if you have a morning flight
- Prepaid taxis at the airport are reliable and reasonably priced
- Avoid unofficial touts who approach you inside the terminal

## Transport from Indore Airport to Ujjain

### Taxi (Recommended)

The most convenient option — door-to-door service from the airport.

- **Prepaid taxi at the airport:** Available at the counter in the arrival hall. Sedan to Ujjain: Rs.800-Rs.1,200. SUV: Rs.1,200-Rs.2,000.
- **Ola/Uber:** Book via the app after you land. Outstation options available. Sedan: Rs.800-Rs.1,500.
- **Local taxi operators:** Outside the terminal — compare rates and negotiate.
- **Travel time:** 1.5-2 hours on NH52 (excellent road condition)
- **Route:** Airport -> NH52 -> direct to Ujjain (no major stops)

### Bus (Economical)

If you want to save money, take a bus from Indore to Ujjain:

1. From Indore Airport, take an Ola/Uber/taxi to **Navlakha Bus Stand** or **Sarwate Bus Stand** in Indore (Rs.200-Rs.400, 20-30 minutes)
2. Board a bus to Ujjain ISBT (Rs.80-Rs.300, 1-1.5 hours)
3. From Ujjain ISBT, take an auto to Mahakaleshwar Temple (Rs.30-Rs.70, 10 minutes)

**Total cost:** Rs.300-Rs.700 per person. Good if you are traveling alone and on a budget.

**Bus operators:** MP Tourism buses, private operators like VRL, and state transport buses run frequently between Indore and Ujjain. The journey is comfortable and the buses are well-maintained.

### Train (Fastest)

If you have time:

1. Take a taxi from Indore Airport to **Indore Junction (INDB)** railway station (Rs.200-Rs.400, 20 minutes)
2. Board an Indore-Ujjain Passenger or Intercity train (Rs.30-Rs.150, 1 hour)
3. From Ujjain Junction, auto to Mahakaleshwar (Rs.30-Rs.80)

**Total cost:** Rs.250-Rs.550. Fastest overall option (about 2 hours from airport to temple).

**Train frequency:** Multiple trains run daily between Indore and Ujjain, including the Indore-Ujjain Intercity Express, the Indore-Ujjain Passenger, and the Jabalpur-Indore Express. Check the IRCTC website or app for current schedules.

## The NH52 Road Journey

The road from Indore Airport to Ujjain via NH52 is one of the best highways in Madhya Pradesh. The road is wide, well-maintained, and has minimal traffic. The journey passes through scenic countryside with fields of soybean, wheat, and cotton.

### Key Landmarks on the Route

- **Rau:** A small town about 10 km from the airport, known for its industrial area
- **Pithampur:** A major industrial hub, about 25 km from Ujjain
- **Dewas:** A historic city about 20 km before Ujjain, with its own temples and ghats
- **Kaliadeh Palace:** A historic palace on the banks of the Shipra River, just before entering Ujjain

The road is safe to drive at any time of day. Night travel is common for pilgrims arriving on late flights.

## Tips for Arriving at Indore Airport

- **Keep cash handy.** Taxi drivers at the airport may not accept cards for short distances. Have Rs.200-Rs.500 in cash for the initial leg.
- **Pre-book your taxi.** If possible, book a taxi from Ujjain to pick you up at the airport. It is more reliable than finding one on arrival during peak hours.
- **Download Ola/Uber.** The app works reliably at IDR. Book before you exit the terminal for a smoother experience.
- **Plan for festival traffic.** During Mahashivratri, Sawan, and other peak pilgrimage periods, traffic on NH52 from Indore to Ujjain can add 30-45 minutes to the journey. Leave extra buffer time.
- **Carry a portable charger.** Long journeys and wait times can drain your phone battery. A portable charger ensures you stay connected.
- **Verify flight schedules.** Flight schedules are subject to change. Check your flight status before leaving for the airport.

## Nearby Attractions: If You Have Time

If you arrive during the day and have a few hours before your temple visit, consider these stops:

- **Kaliadeh Palace:** A 18th-century palace on the banks of the Shipra River, about 10 km from Ujjain. The palace has beautiful architecture and a peaceful atmosphere.
- **Dewas Temples:** The twin temples of Dewas (about 20 km from Ujjain) are dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Chamunda. They are worth a quick visit if you have time.
- **Pithampur:** An industrial area with several large manufacturing units. Not a tourist attraction, but notable if you are interested in India's industrial landscape.

## FAQs

**Q: Is there a direct bus from Indore Airport to Ujjain?**
A: No direct bus service is available from the airport terminal. You will need to take a taxi or auto to Indore city and then board a bus to Ujjain.

**Q: How much does a taxi from Indore Airport to Ujjain cost?**
A: A sedan taxi costs Rs.800-Rs.1,500 depending on the service (prepaid counter, Ola/Uber, or local operator). SUV options are Rs.1,200-Rs.2,000.

**Q: How long is the journey from Indore Airport to Ujjain?**
A: 1.5-2 hours by direct taxi on NH52. By bus, 2.5-3 hours (including the Indore city leg). By train, about 2 hours total including the airport-to-station taxi.
