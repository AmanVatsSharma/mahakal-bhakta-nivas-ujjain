## One Big Room Beats Two Hotel Bookings

The default instinct on booking apps is to search for two double rooms. For a family of four or five, that is usually the worse deal on every count:

- **Cost:** two hotel rooms almost always cost more in total than one 3 or 4 bed dharamshala room
- **Logistics:** separate rooms mean separate keys, separate check-ins and splitting the family across corridors or floors
- **Night-time care:** with grandparents and children in the same room, someone waking unwell at 2 AM is noticed immediately
- **Morning coordination:** everyone dresses, eats and leaves for darshan together instead of waiting on each other

A family **dharamshala room in Ujjain** near the temple is built around exactly this pattern of travel — multi-generational, early-rising, meal-oriented.
