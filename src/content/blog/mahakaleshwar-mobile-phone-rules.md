# Mahakaleshwar Temple Mobile Phone Rules: Photography, Cloak Room & What to Bring

One of the most common questions first-time visitors to Mahakaleshwar ask is: **"Can I take my phone inside the temple?"** The answer is nuanced — mobile phones are allowed in most areas of the temple complex, but not in the sacred inner sanctum.

This guide explains the complete Mahakaleshwar Temple mobile phone policy, photography rules, cloak room facilities, and what you should leave in your room before heading to the temple.

## Mobile Phone Policy at Mahakaleshwar

### Where Phones Are NOT Allowed

**Inside the Garbhagriha (Sanctum Sanctorum):**
- The main shrine where the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga is located
- No mobile phones, cameras, or any electronic devices
- You must deposit ALL electronics at the cloak room before entering

**During Bhasma Aarti (4 AM):**
- Strictly no phones or cameras during the aarti ceremony
- Security is extra vigilant during this time
- Even phones in pockets are not tolerated

### Where Phones Are Allowed

**Outer Courtyard:**
- Mobile phones are permitted in the main courtyard
- You can use your phone for photos (respectfully) and communication
- However, keep your phone on silent/vibrate mode

**Temple Complex Perimeter:**
- Outside the main temple building, phones are freely allowed
- You can take photos of the temple exterior, the corridors, and the surrounding area

**Nearby Areas:**
- The market area outside the temple and the corridor area allow full phone usage

## Cloak Room Facilities

### How the Cloak Room Works

The cloak room (also called the **baggage counter**) at Mahakaleshwar is a secure facility where you can deposit your phone, camera, and other prohibited items before entering the inner temple.

**Location:** Near the main entrance gate of Mahakaleshwar Temple

**Operating Hours:** 4 AM to 9:30 PM daily

**Process:**
1. Approach the cloak room counter with your items
2. Show your phone/camera for verification
3. Receive a numbered token/tag
4. Security may check your pockets (standard procedure)
5. Deposit your items in the designated area
6. Keep the token safe — you'll need it to retrieve your items
7. Enter the temple

**Retrieving Your Items:**
1. Show your token at the cloak room
2. Verify your phone/camera matches the token number
3. Collect your items

### Cloak Room Charges

The cloak room at Mahakaleshwar is typically **free or charges a nominal fee**:

| Item Type | Fee (approx.) |
|-----------|---------------|
| Mobile phone | ₹5–₹10 |
| Camera | ₹10–₹20 |
| Bag/suitcase | ₹20–₹50 |
| Combined (phone + wallet) | ₹10–₹20 |

### Cloak Room Safety Tips

1. **Remove the SIM card** from your phone before depositing (optional but recommended)
2. **Switch off your phone** before depositing
3. **Take a photo of your phone** before depositing (for reference if there's a dispute)
4. **Keep the token in a safe place** — don't lose it
5. **Don't deposit cash** in your phone case — keep your wallet with you
6. **Arrive early** — the cloak room can get crowded before major aartis

## Photography Policy

### Where Photography is NOT Allowed

- **Garbhagriha (inner sanctum):** Strictly prohibited — no exceptions
- **During Bhasma Aarti:** Strictly prohibited — devotees are asked to put away all devices
- **During Mangla Aarti (early morning):** Discouraged but sometimes tolerated

### Where Photography is Allowed

- **Outer courtyard and corridors:** Permitted, but be respectful
- **Temple exterior:** Fully permitted
- **Ghats and river areas:** Permitted
- **Other temples in Ujjain (Gopal Mandir, Harsiddhi, etc.):** Generally permitted, but check at each temple

### Photography Etiquette

1. **Ask before photographing people** — some devotees may object
2. **No selfie sticks** — they're disruptive and often banned
3. **No flash photography** inside the temple
4. **Don't block pathways** for other devotees while taking photos
5. **Keep moving** — don't hold up the queue for a photo

## Security Procedures at the Temple

### Temple Security Check

Before entering Mahakaleshwar Temple, you'll go through a security check:

1. **Metal detector** — standard walk-through
2. **Bag check** — bags are visually inspected or X-rayed
3. **Pocket check** — security may ask you to empty pockets
4. **Phone check** — ensure your phone is in the cloak room before reaching security

**Tips:**
- Remove belts, watches, and metal items before the metal detector
- Keep your ID and small cash in a pocket you can easily access (security may check)
- Cooperate fully with security — they are ensuring everyone's safety

## What to Carry vs. What to Leave Behind

### Bring to the Temple:
- Small wallet with cash (₹100–₹500 for prasad, donations)
- Valid ID (Aadhaar card or passport)
- Water bottle (plastic is fine)
- Handkerchief
- Your cloak room token

### Leave at Your Room:
- Mobile phone (use cloak room)
- Camera
- Large bags/backpacks
- Selfie sticks
- Expensive jewelry
- Perfume/spray bottles

## Special Situations

### During Bhasma Aarti (4 AM)
- ALL electronics must be deposited at the cloak room
- Even devices in pockets are not allowed
- Arrive by 3:30 AM to have enough time at the cloak room and security
- Book your Bhasma Aarti pass in advance via the [MP Devasthan portal](https://www.mptourism.com)

### During Mahashivratri
- Extra security is deployed
- Even stricter rules for bags and electronics
- Arrive 2 hours early to account for extended security checks
- See our [Mahashivratri guide](/blog/mahashivratri-mahakaleshwar-guide.md) for festival-specific tips

### During Kumbh Mela (Simhastha)
- Expect the most stringent security checks
- Large bags may not be allowed at all
- Plan accordingly and travel light to the temple

## FAQs

**Q1: Can I keep my phone in my pocket while entering the temple?**
No, you must deposit your phone at the cloak room before entering the garbhagriha. Security will check pockets.

**Q2: What happens if I'm caught with a phone inside?**
It will be confiscated and you may be asked to leave the temple premises.

**Q3: Is there WiFi available at Mahakaleshwar Temple?**
No, there is no public WiFi inside the temple complex.

**Q4: Can I take photos of the temple exterior?**
Yes, photography of the temple exterior and outer courtyard is allowed. Just be respectful of other devotees.

**Q5: Are there lockers for bags only (without electronics)?**
Yes, the cloak room accepts all types of bags and luggage, not just electronics.

**Q6: What time does the cloak room close?**
The cloak room operates until 9:30 PM. Make sure to collect your items before closing time.

**Q7: Is the cloak room secure?**
Yes, the cloak room is managed by temple authorities with token-based retrieval. However, always take a photo of your phone before depositing it as a precaution.

**Q8: Can I use my phone for online darshan booking at the temple?**
Yes, mobile phone usage is allowed in the outer courtyard and ticket counters for online bookings. You just can't carry it inside the inner sanctum.
