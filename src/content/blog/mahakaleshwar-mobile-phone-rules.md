faq:
- q: Are mobile phones allowed inside Mahakaleshwar Temple?
  a: Mobile phones are NOT allowed in the main garbhagriha (sanctum sanctorum) where
    the Jyotirlinga is located. They are allowed in the outer courtyard and temple
    complex. You must deposit your phone at the cloak room before entering the inner
    temple.
- q: Is photography allowed at Mahakaleshwar?
  a: Photography is strictly prohibited in the garbhagriha and during Bhasma Aarti.
    Photography is allowed in the outer courtyard, but be respectful and avoid photographing
    other devotees without permission.
