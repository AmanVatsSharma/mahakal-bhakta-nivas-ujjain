Mumbai to Ujjain is one of the most travelled pilgrimage routes in central India. Lakhs of Mahadev bhakts make the journey every year, drawn by Mahakaleshwar — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and the most spiritually significant Shiva temple in Ujjain. The journey itself is straightforward, with multiple options depending on your budget, schedule, and comfort preferences.

This guide covers every travel option — flight, train, and road — so you can choose the route that fits your needs, and plan your arrival in Ujjain with confidence.

## The Route in Numbers

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Road distance** | 650–700 km (via Mumbai–Indore Expressway, NH 52) |
| **Straight-line distance** | ~570 km |
| **Train journey time** | 10–18 hours |
| **Flight time (Mumbai to Indore)** | ~1 hour |
| **Indore to Ujjain by road** | 1.5–2 hours |
| **Total door-to-door by flight** | 4–5 hours |
| **Total door-to-door by train** | 10–20 hours (including travel to/from stations) |
| **Total door-to-door by road** | 10–12 hours |

## Option 1: Train (The Popular Choice)

Trains are the default option for most Mumbai–Ujjain travellers. The route is direct, well-serviced, and offers a range of classes from general unreserved to fully air-conditioned.

### Direct Trains from Mumbai to Ujjain Junction (UJN)

| Train Name | Number | Class Options | Departs Mumbai | Arrives Ujjain | Journey Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Avantika Express** | 12961 / 12962 | 1A, 2A, 3A, SL, 2S | Daily | Daily | ~13–14 hours |
| **Dayodaya Express** | 12967 / 12968 | 1A, 2A, 3A, SL | Multiple days | Multiple days | ~12–13 hours |
| **Intercity Express** | 12415 / 12416 | 1A, 2A, 3A, CC, 2S | Most days | Most days | ~10–11 hours |
| **Lashkar Express** | 11125 / 11126 | 1A, 2A, 3A, SL | Multiple days | Multiple days | ~14–15 hours |

**Departure stations in Mumbai:** Most trains depart from **Mumbai Central (BCT)** or **Dadar (DR)**. A few services depart from **Bandra Terminus (BDTS)**. Check your ticket carefully before heading to the station.

**Ujjain Junction (UJN)** is the arrival station. It is 2–3 km from the temple — a 10-minute auto-rickshaw ride. Our [Mahakaleshwar darshan guide](/blog/mahakaleshwar-darshan-guide/) covers the route from the station to the temple gate in detail.

### Train Class Comparison

| Class | Features | Comfort Level | Typical Fare (Mumbai–Ujjain) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1A (First AC)** | Private coupes, bedding, meals | Highest | ₹3,000–₹5,000 |
| **2A (2-Tier AC)** | Two-tier AC, bedding, curtains | Very High | ₹2,000–₹3,000 |
| **3A (3-Tier AC)** | Three-tier AC, bedding | Good | ₹1,200–₹2,000 |
| **SL (Sleeper)** | Non-AC sleeper, bedding on request | Moderate | ₹600–₹1,000 |
| **2S (Second Seating)** | Non-AC chair car, no bedding | Basic | ₹400–₹700 |

**Recommendation for pilgrims:** 3A (3-Tier AC) is the sweet spot for overnight travel — comfortable, reasonably priced, and with bedding included. Avoid 2S if you are travelling overnight; you will want to sleep.

### How to Book Train Tickets

- **IRCTC website** (www.irctc.co.in) — the official platform. Create an account, search for Mumbai to Ujjain (UJN), and book in advance. IRCTC requires registration and sometimes KYC — set this up a week before you plan to book.
- **IRCTC Rail Connect app** — mobile-friendly version of the IRCTC experience.
- **Travel agents** — many agents in Mumbai and Ujjain handle train bookings for a small fee.
- **Tatkal scheme** — last-minute booking opens one day before departure (4 AM for AC, 8 AM for non-AC). Tatkal seats fill quickly, especially for popular trains during festivals. Not recommended for Mahashivratri — book in advance instead.

## Option 2: Flight + Road (The Fastest Route)

Flying is the fastest way to cover the distance, but it is not a direct Mumbai–Ujjain route because Ujjain has no commercial airport. The standard flight route is:

**Mumbai → Indore (Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar International Airport, IDR) → Ujjain by road**

### Mumbai to Indore Flights

Multiple daily flights connect Mumbai (BOM) to Indore (IDR):

| Airline | Typical Departure | Flight Time | Daily Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IndiGo** | Multiple times/day | ~1 hr 05 min | 4–6 daily |
| **SpiceJet** | Multiple times/day | ~1 hr 05 min | 2–3 daily |
| **Air India** | Morning/Afternoon | ~1 hr 15 min | 2–3 daily |

**Best booking tips:**
- Book 4–6 weeks in advance for the best fares (often ₹2,500–₹4,000 one-way)
- Last-minute bookings can be ₹7,000–₹12,000 during peak periods
- Use Google Flights or Skyscanner for comparison across airlines

### Indore to Ujjain by Road

Once you land in Indore, reaching Ujjain is straightforward:

| Mode | Time | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taxi (pre-booked)** | 1.5–2 hrs | ₹1,200–₹2,500 | Most comfortable; book in advance |
| **Shared taxi / Ola / Uber** | 1.5–2 hrs | ₹600–₹1,200 per person | Good for solo travellers; available at the airport |
| **Bus (MPSRTC Volvo)** | 2–2.5 hrs | ₹250–₹500 per person | Budget-friendly; buses depart from the Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar Bus Stand |
| **Private car rental** | 1.5–2 hrs | ₹2,000–₹4,000 per day | Best for families or groups |

**Total cost estimate (flight + taxi):** ₹4,000–₹8,000 per person door-to-door.

## Option 3: Road Trip

Driving from Mumbai to Ujjain gives you maximum flexibility and is a scenic route through central India.

### The Route

The most direct route via the **Mumbai–Indore Expressway (NH 52)**:

**Mumbai → Surat → Vadodara → Indore → Ujjain**

Total distance: approximately 650–700 km.

| Segment | Distance | Approx. Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mumbai to Surat | ~280 km | 4–5 hours |
| Surat to Vadodara | ~130 km | 2–2.5 hours |
| Vadodara to Indore | ~380 km | 5–6 hours |
| Indore to Ujjain | ~55 km | 1–1.5 hours |

### Road Trip Tips

- **Fuel stops:** Petrol pumps are frequent on NH 52. Keep the tank above quarter-full.
- **Rest stops:** Vadodara and Indore are the best places for a meal break — both have good restaurants.
- **Night driving:** The expressway is well-lit and generally safe, but daytime driving is recommended for first-time travellers on this route.
- **Speed:** NH 52 has a speed limit of 80 km/h for most of the expressway stretch. Expect tolls on the Mumbai–Indore Expressway segment.
- **Car rental:** If you do not own a car, consider a self-drive rental from Zoomcar, Revv, or Myles in Mumbai. Prices start from around ₹2,500/day for a compact car.

## Option 4: Bus

State-run and private buses connect Mumbai to Indore, and from Indore you can take a bus to Ujjain. This is the most budget-friendly option:

| Service | Route | Approx. Time | Approx. Fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MPSRTC Volvo** | Mumbai → Indore | 12–14 hours | ₹800–₹1,500 |
| **Private operators** | Mumbai → Indore | 11–13 hours | ₹1,000–₹2,000 |
| **MPSRTC Volvo** | Indore → Ujjain | 2–2.5 hours | ₹250–₹500 |

**Total journey time:** 15–17 hours. **Total cost:** ₹1,050–₹2,500.

**Considerations:** Bus travel is the slowest option and is tiring for a journey of this length. It works well for budget travellers who have flexible schedules. The Mumbai to Indore overnight buses are reasonably comfortable (Volvo or AC sleeper). The Indore to Ujjain leg is short and manageable.

## What to Pack for the Journey

Regardless of which option you choose, here is a checklist for the Mumbai–Ujjain journey:

- **Light woollens:** Ujjain can be cool in winter (November–February). Even in summer, the early morning and evening are pleasant.
- **Comfortable walking shoes:** Ujjain involves significant walking — temple queues, ghats, Mahakal Lok corridor.
- **ID proof:** Carry your original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport) for hotel check-in, darshan passes, and train/flight booking verification.
- **Cash:** ATMs are available in Ujjain, but some temple offerings, auto-rickshaws, and small vendors prefer cash. Carry ₹2,000–₹3,000 in small denominations.
- **Power bank:** Your phone is your camera, map, and booking confirmation. Keep it charged.
- **Dry snacks and water:** Especially for train travellers. Ujjain has good food options, but for the journey itself, pack what you need.
- **A small offering pouch:** Flowers, coconut, or prasad for Mahakaleshwar. You can also buy offerings near the temple, but carrying a small personal offering adds to the experience.

## Practical Tips for a Smooth Journey

### Booking in Advance

Festival periods in Ujjain — Mahashivratri, Shravan month, and Kumbh Mela — see a surge in travel demand from Mumbai and across India. Book your train tickets 60–90 days ahead on IRCTC, and book flight tickets 4–6 weeks in advance. During Mahashivratri, accommodation in Ujjain also fills up 6–8 weeks ahead. Our [Mahakal Bhakta Nivas rooms](/rooms/) are a reliable option for pilgrims, but early booking is essential.

### At Mumbai's Railway Stations

Mumbai Central and Dadar are bustling stations. Arrive at least 30 minutes before departure for unreserved or 2S tickets, and 45 minutes if you have a reserved berth. Carry a printed or digital copy of your ticket and a valid photo ID. Both stations have food stalls, drinking water, and waiting rooms.

### At Mumbai's Airport

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is well-connected to the city by taxi, metro, and Uber/Ola. For early-morning flights to Indore, stay near the airport the night before or allow at least 3 hours from South Mumbai to check-in. Domestic flights in India require a valid photo ID at the security checkpoint.

### Food on the Journey

- **By train:** IRCTC e-catering is available on most long-distance trains. You can order meals through the IRCTC app. Pantry cars are available on the Avantika Express and Dayodaya Express. Alternatively, pack home-style snacks and fruits.
- **By flight:** Indore airport has a decent food court. After landing, stop for a quick meal before the 1.5–2 hour road journey to Ujjain.
- **By road:** Vadodara and Indore are excellent meal-break cities. Both offer everything from roadside Gujarati thalis to fine dining.

### Safety Tips

- Keep your belongings zipped and within sight on trains and buses. Avoid displaying valuables.
- Use only licensed taxis or app-based cabs (Ola, Uber) at Indore airport and Ujjain Junction.
- If driving at night, take a co-driver and plan rest stops every 2–3 hours.
- Keep emergency numbers handy: Mumbai railway helpline 139, Indore airport assistance, and Ujjain police 100.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is the distance between Mumbai and Ujjain?

**A:** The road distance is approximately 650–700 km via the Mumbai–Indore Expressway and NH 52. The aerial (straight-line) distance is around 570 km.

### Q: How long does it take to travel from Mumbai to Ujjain?

**A:** By train: 10–18 hours depending on the service. By flight: approximately 1 hour to Indore, plus 1.5–2 hours by road to Ujjain — total journey time of 4–5 hours door-to-door. By road: approximately 10–12 hours by car or bus.

### Q: Which is the best way to travel from Mumbai to Ujjain?

**A:** It depends on your priorities. Trains offer comfort and the direct option from Mumbai to Ujjain Junction (no change required). Flights to Indore are fastest but require a connecting road journey. For budget travellers, the train is usually the most practical choice.

### Q: Are there direct trains from Mumbai to Ujjain?

**A:** Yes — Ujjain Junction (UJN) is directly connected to Mumbai Central and Dadar by multiple express trains, including the Avantika Express, the Dayodaya Express, and the Intercity Express. No change is required.

### Q: Are there direct flights from Mumbai to Ujjain?

**A:** No — Ujjain does not have a commercial airport. The nearest airport is Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar International Airport in Indore (IDR), approximately 55 km from Ujjain by road (1.5–2 hours). Several daily flights operate between Mumbai and Indore.

### Q: Can I hire a car or self-drive from Mumbai to Ujjain?

**A:** Yes — the route via the Mumbai–Indore Expressway (NH 52) is well-maintained and takes approximately 10–12 hours. Night driving on the expressway is generally safe, but daytime driving offers better visibility and more rest-stop options.

### Q: What is the best time of year to travel from Mumbai to Ujjain?

**A:** October to March offers the best weather — cool, dry, and comfortable for both the train journey and the Ujjain visit. Avoid the monsoon season (July–September) if possible, as heavy rain can delay trains and make the Shipra-side areas muddy. Mahashivratri season (February–March) is the most spiritually rewarding but also the most crowded — book well in advance.

**A:** Yes. Especially during festival periods (Mahashivratri, Shravan, Kumbh Mela), trains between Mumbai and Ujjain fill up days before departure. Book 60–90 days in advance on the IRCTC website for the best seat and berth availability.
