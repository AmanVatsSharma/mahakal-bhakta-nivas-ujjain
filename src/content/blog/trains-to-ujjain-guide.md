Ujjain Junction (UJN) is more than just a railway station — for most pilgrims, it is the gateway to Mahakaleshwar. Located just 3 km from the temple, it is the most practical way to reach Ujjain by train from anywhere in India. This guide covers trains from major cities, station facilities, and how to get from the station to the temple.

---

## Ujjain Junction Railway Station: Overview

**Station Code:** UJN  
**Zone:** Western Railway  
**Location:** Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh  
**Distance from Mahakaleshwar Temple:** 3 km  
**Platforms:** 5 platforms (growing with ongoing expansion)

Ujjain Junction lies on the Delhi–Mumbai and Indore–Bhopal rail corridors, making it a well-connected station with direct trains from most major Indian cities. The station is clean, has basic amenities, and is surrounded by auto-rickshaws and taxis the moment you step out.

## Key Trains to Ujjain from Major Cities

### From Delhi

| Train | Type | From | Frequency | Journey Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ujjain Express | Mail/Express | Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM) | Daily | ~14 hours |
| Dakshin Express | Superfast | Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM) | Daily | ~13 hours (to Bhopal, then connect) |

The **Ujjain Express** is the most popular direct train. It departs Delhi in the evening and reaches Ujjain the next morning — ideal for starting your darshan day fresh. Book 2–4 weeks in advance via IRCTC.

### From Mumbai

| Train | Type | From | Frequency | Journey Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mumbai–Ujjain Express | Mail/Express | Dadar (DR) | Weekly | ~14 hours |
| Avantika Express | Superfast | Mumbai (LTT) | Daily | ~14 hours (to Bhopal, then connect) |

Check IRCTC for the latest Mumbai–Ujjain direct services. The Avantika Express is a reliable daily option if a direct Mumbai–Ujjain train is not available on your date.

### From Indore

| Train | Type | Journey Time | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indore–Ujjain Passenger | Passenger | ~1 hour | Daily |
| Indore–Ujjain Intercity | Express | ~1 hour | Daily |

The **1-hour train** from Indore is the fastest way to reach Ujjain from anywhere in MP. Trains run every 2–3 hours throughout the day. Perfect for a day trip or as a leg of a longer journey.

### From Bhopal

| Train | Type | Journey Time | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bhopal–Ujjain Passenger | Passenger | ~3 hours | Daily |
| Bhopal–Ujjain Intercity | Express | ~2.5–3 hours | Daily |

Direct trains run multiple times a day. The Intercity Express is faster and more comfortable. Book in advance during festivals.

### From Ahmedabad

| Train | Type | Journey Time | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ahmedabad–Ujjain Express | Express | ~10–11 hours | Weekly |

The weekly Ahmedabad–Ujjain Express is the main direct service. For more options, connect via Ratlam or Indore.

### From Jaipur

| Train | Type | Journey Time | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaipur–Ujjain Express | Express | ~11–12 hours | Weekly |

Connecting via Ajmer or Kota is another option — more frequent trains on those legs.

## Station Facilities

Ujjain Junction provides:

- **Waiting rooms** — AC and non-AC, available on a first-come-first-served basis (small fee for non-AC, higher for AC)
- **Retiring rooms** — For longer waits, book at the reservation counter
- **Cloakroom** — Available near platform 1. ₹20–₹50 for 24 hours depending on luggage size
- **Food stalls** — IRCTC food plaza, local vendors selling snacks, tea, and packed meals
- **ATMs** — SBI and other bank ATMs near the station entrance
- **Inquiry counter** — For train status, PNR inquiries, and reservation assistance
- **Prepaid taxi counter** — Available near the main exit
- **Book stall** — Near platform 1, sells magazines, snacks, and travel essentials

## From Ujjain Junction to Mahakaleshwar Temple

**Distance:** 3 km  
**Travel time:** 10–15 minutes

### Auto-Rickshaw

The most common and economical option. Auto-rickshaws line up outside the station exit. Options:

- **Metered auto:** ₹30–₹50 depending on exact drop point (insist on the meter)
- **Fixed rate:** Drivers may quote ₹60–₹100 for the temple — negotiate down or walk to a different auto
- **Shared auto:** ₹10–₹20 per person, drops multiple passengers along the route

### Taxi

Prepaid taxis at the station counter or via Ola/Uber:

- **Sedan:** ₹150–₹250 to the temple
- **SUV:** ₹250–₹400
- **Prepaid counter** at the station gives you a fixed-rate receipt — the most hassle-free option

## Night Train Considerations

If you are arriving at Ujjain Junction late at night (after 11:00 PM), here is what to expect:

- Auto-rickshaws are available but may quote higher rates after midnight (₹100–₹150)
- The temple is closed between 11:00 PM and 4:00 AM — you cannot enter for darshan at night
- Book a dharamshala near the temple in advance so you can rest and head out for morning darshan at 6:00 AM
- The station is safe, but do not loiter with valuables visible

Arriving late actually works well — sleep, wake up at 6:00 AM, and do the calmest darshan of the day before the crowds build.

## Booking Tips

1. **Book 2–4 weeks in advance** for regular travel; 6–8 weeks for festival periods (Mahashivratri, Nag Panchami, Sawan)
2. **Use IRCTC** (irctc.co.in) — the official platform. Third-party apps and websites often charge convenience fees
3. **Tatkal quota** opens one day before: 10:00 AM for AC classes, 8:00 AM for non-AC. Useful if you are traveling on short notice, but seats are limited
4. **Check PNR status** regularly after booking — waitlisted tickets sometimes clear as the travel date approaches
5. **Download the NTES app** or use the IRCTC app for real-time train status and platform information
