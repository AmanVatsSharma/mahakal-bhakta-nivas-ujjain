Two Jyotirlingas, 140 kilometres apart — the Ujjain–Omkareshwar leg is the most-travelled Jyotirlinga pairing in India, and it fits in one determined day. Here is exactly how to run it.

## The Drive: Distance, Time, Routes

| Route | Distance | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Ujjain → Omkareshwar (via Indore-side roads) | ~140 km | 3–3.5 hrs |
| Ujjain → Omkareshwar (via Mhow–Khandwa side) | ~140–160 km | 3.5–4 hrs |

Both are Malwa-plateau drives through small towns and farmland. Conditions vary — let your driver choose the day's better road, or ask us at the desk when you book; we send this route every week ([contact](/contact/)). Buses run toward the Khandwa side with transfers; a **hired cab for the day** is what most families choose, and groups often share tempo-travellers.

## The Same-Day Plan (Proven Version)

- **7:00 AM** — leave Ujjain after early breakfast (ask for a packed/early one — ours runs early on request, [amenities](/amenities/))
- **10:30–11:00 AM** — arrive Omkareshwar ghat side
- **11:00 AM** — **Omkareshwar darshan** (main island shrine)
- **12:30 PM** — cross to **Mamleshwar** on the opposite bank — the tradition completing the darshan ([why both matter](/blog/omkareshwar-vs-mahakaleshwar/))
- **1:30 PM** — lunch near the ghats
- **2:30 PM** — **island parikrama** (~2–3 hrs) *or* boat ride + rest if saving legs
- **5:00 PM** — depart for Ujjain
- **8:00–8:30 PM** — back in Ujjain; in time even for a late Sandhya Aarti stroll

**With Bhasma Aarti the next morning?** This plan gets you to bed by 9:30 PM — the pairing works beautifully: Mahakal at dawn, Omkareshwar by day.

## The Overnight Alternative

If your pace is gentler, sleep at Omkareshwar: ghat-side rooms, the **Narmada aarti** in the evening, and a misty morning parikrama before returning. The trade is one extra hotel night vs a long day — with elders, the overnight usually wins.

## What Not to Miss at Omkareshwar

1. **The island's Om shape** — visible in map/river view; you walk *inside* the symbol
2. **Parikrama path** — stone steps, shrines big and small, river views ([what the walk means](/blog/omkareshwar-vs-mahakaleshwar/))
3. **Mamleshwar** — the serene opposite-bank temple, many devotees' favourite of the two
4. **Boat rides** — short, lovely, and easy on the knees
