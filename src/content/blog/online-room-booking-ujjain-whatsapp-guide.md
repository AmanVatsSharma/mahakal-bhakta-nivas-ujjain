## Why Direct WhatsApp Booking Beats OTA Platforms

Many pilgrims rely on OTA platforms like MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, or OYO for booking rooms. While these platforms are convenient, they come with significant drawbacks that WhatsApp booking avoids:

- **No Markup Pricing**: OTA platforms add 15–30% commission on top of the actual room rate. When you book directly via WhatsApp, you get the same or better room at a lower price
- **No Cancellation Hassles**: OTA cancellations often involve platform rules, refund delays, and customer support queues. Direct booking means you speak to the owner directly for any changes
- **Better Room Allocation**: Properties often allocate their best rooms to direct bookings, reserving lesser rooms for OTA guests
- **Personal Relationship**: Building a relationship with the property means better treatment on repeat visits and priority during peak seasons
- **Flexibility**: Discuss special requests directly — early check-in, late check-out, extra bedding, specific floor preferences — without platform restrictions

## What a Real Confirmation Looks Like

A legitimate booking confirmation via WhatsApp should include:
- Property name and address clearly stated
- Room type and number
- Dates and nights confirmed
- Total cost and advance paid
- Payment method used
- Contact person name and phone
- Cancellation policy mentioned
- Check-in/check-out timings

If any of these details are missing, ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Avoiding Booking Scams

While WhatsApp booking is generally safe, be cautious of these red flags:
- The contact asks for full payment before showing any room details
- No physical address is provided
- The number is new and has no online presence
- Prices seem significantly lower than market rates
- No cancellation policy is mentioned

Always verify the property through Google Maps, check reviews, and confirm the address before making any payment.

## Day-Use Rooms

If you are traveling on the same day and need a room for a few hours (for bathing, changing, resting, or storing luggage), ask about day-use rooms. Many properties near Mahakaleshwar offer rooms for 4–8 hours at 50–70% of the nightly rate. This is especially useful if you arrive by morning train and depart by evening.

## Cancellation and Flexibility

Understand the cancellation policy before confirming your booking. Typical policies include:
- Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in
- 50% refund for cancellations 24–48 hours before check-in
- No refund for cancellations within 24 hours of check-in
- No-show = full charge

During peak seasons (Mahashivratri, Sawan), cancellation policies may be stricter. Always confirm the policy in writing via WhatsApp so you have a record.
