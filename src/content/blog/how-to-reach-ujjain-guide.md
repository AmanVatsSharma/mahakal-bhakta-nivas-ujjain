Getting to Ujjain for Mahakaleshwar darshan is straightforward — the city is well-connected by train, road, and air (via nearby Indore). Here is everything you need to know about every transport option, so you can plan your journey with confidence.

---

## By Train

Ujjain Junction (station code: **UJN**) is the city's main railway station and the most convenient way to reach Ujjain for most pilgrims. It sits on the Western Railway network, connecting Ujjain to Delhi, Mumbai, Bhopal, Indore, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, and several other cities.

### Key Trains to Ujjain

| Train | From | Frequency | Journey Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ujjain Express | Delhi (Nizamuddin) | Daily | ~14 hours |
| Indore–Ujjain Passenger | Indore | Daily | ~1 hour |
| Bhopal–Ujjain Passenger | Bhopal | Daily | ~3 hours |
| Mumbai–Ujjain Express | Mumbai (Dadar) | Daily | ~14 hours |
| Jaipur–Ujjain Express | Jaipur | Weekly | ~11 hours |
| Ahmedabad–Ujjain Express | Ahmedabad | Weekly | ~10 hours |

Book through IRCTC (irctc.co.in) at least 2–4 weeks in advance for festival periods like Mahashivratri, Nag Panchami, or Sawan. Tatkal tickets open one day before travel.

### From Ujjain Junction to Mahakaleshwar

The temple is **3 km** from Ujjain Junction. Auto-rickshaws are available outside the station (₹30–₹80 to the temple), and prepaid taxis are also an option. The ride takes 10–15 minutes. During peak hours, shared autos operate at ₹10–₹20 per person. For a detailed breakdown, see our [Ujjain Station to Mahakaleshwar guide](/blog/ujjain-station-to-mahakaleshwar-guide/).

## By Bus

Ujjain has a well-developed bus network. The **Inter State Bus Terminal (ISBT)** is located near the city center, about 2.5 km from Mahakaleshwar Temple.

### Buses from Major Cities

- **Indore to Ujjain:** Every 30 minutes from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Volvo and ordinary buses. Journey: 1–1.5 hours. Fare: ₹80–₹300.
- **Bhopal to Ujjain:** Every 1–2 hours. Ordinary and AC buses. Journey: 3–4 hours. Fare: ₹120–₹450.
- **Nagda to Ujjain:** Frequent local buses. Journey: 45 minutes–1 hour.
- **Ahmedabad to Ujjain:** Overnight and day buses available. Journey: 7–8 hours.
- **Mumbai to Ujjain:** Overnight Volvo buses. Journey: 12–14 hours.

State-run MP Roadways and private operators (including Volvo services from private operators) both serve the route. Book via the MP government portal (mpbus.in) or RedBus/AbhiBus. For the ISBT-to-temple leg, see our [Ujjain Bus Stand guide](/blog/ujjain-bus-stand-guide/).

## By Flight

The nearest airport to Ujjain is **Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport (IDR) in Indore**, approximately **55 km** away. It is a modern airport with excellent connectivity.

### Flight Connectivity

| From | Major Airlines | Approx. Flight Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mumbai | IndiGo, Air India, Vistara | 1 hour 15 min |
| Delhi | IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet | 1 hour 30 min |
| Bhopal | IndiGo | 40 min |
| Ahmedabad | IndiGo, Air India | 1 hour |
| Hyderabad | IndiGo | 1 hour 15 min |

## By Self-Drive

Driving to Ujjain is a pleasant experience, especially from Indore. The highways are well-maintained and the route is scenic in parts.

### Key Distances

| From | Distance | Drive Time |
|---|---|---|
| Indore | 55 km | 1 hour (via NH52) |
| Bhopal | 180 km | 3–3.5 hours (via NH46) |
| Ahmedabad | 400 km | 7–8 hours (via NH47) |
| Mumbai | 650 km | 12–14 hours (via NH52) |
| Kota | 260 km | 5–6 hours (via NH76) |

The **NH52** from Indore is a four-lane toll road — excellent driving conditions. The **NH46** from Bhopal is also good, with a mix of four-lane and two-lane stretches. Fuel stations and food stops are plentiful along both routes. For a detailed driving guide with route maps and tips, see [Driving to Ujjain: Road Trip Guide](/blog/driving-to-ujjain-road-trip-guide/).

## Local Transport from Each Arrival Point to Mahakaleshwar

No matter how you arrive, you need to get from the arrival point to Mahakaleshwar Temple. Here is the quick summary:

| Arrival Point | Distance to Temple | Best Transport |
|---|---|---|
| Ujjain Junction | 3 km | Auto (₹30–₹80), Taxi (₹150–₹250) |
| Ujjain ISBT | 2.5 km | Auto (₹30–₹70), Local bus |
| Indore Airport (IDR) | 60 km | Taxi (₹800–₹1,500), Bus from Indore |
| Indore Railway Station | 55 km | Taxi, Bus, or Train to Ujjain |
| Nagda Junction | 45 km | Taxi or shared auto |

Auto-rickshaws in Ujjain are generally metered, but many drivers quote a fixed rate for temple trips. Agree on the fare before you get in, or insist on the meter. Prepaid taxis at the railway station and airport are the most reliable option if you want to avoid haggling.

## Practical Tips

- **Book return transport in advance.** If you are arriving by train or bus for a one-day visit, confirm your return ticket before you head to the temple — queues for evening buses and trains can be long.
- **Carry cash.** Many auto drivers and small shops do not accept cards or UPI. Keep ₹500–₹1,000 in cash for local transport and prasad.
- **Start early.** The 6:00 AM darshan window is the most peaceful and the easiest to reach from any arrival point by taxi.
- **Stay near the temple.** If you are visiting for more than a few hours, [book a room near Mahakaleshwar](/rooms/) and walk to the temple. It transforms the experience.
