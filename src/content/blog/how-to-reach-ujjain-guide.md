Getting to the Lord of Time's city is easier than most pilgrims expect — Ujjain sits on solid rail and road arteries, with Indore's airport doing the aerial work. Here is every option, honestly compared.

## By Air: Fly Into Indore

Ujjain has no commercial airport of its own. The gateway is **Devi Ahilyabai Holkar International Airport, Indore (IDR)** — well connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Jaipur and more.

| Leg | Distance/Time | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Indore airport → Ujjain | ~60 km / 1.5 hrs | Prepaid/private taxi (comfortable), app cabs |
| Indore airport → Indore city → Ujjain | ~2–2.5 hrs total | City bus/cab to Indore, then intercity bus or train |

**Tip:** if arriving late evening, consider staying in Ujjain the same night anyway — the temple quarter is 90 calm minutes away, and morning darshan rewards the early base. ([Where to stay](/blog/where-to-stay-near-mahakaleshwar-temple/).)

## By Train: Ujjain Junction

Ujjain (UJN) is a substantial junction with broad-gauge connectivity in several directions. Typical direct connections include:

- **Indore** (frequent, ~2–3 hrs) and **Bhopal** (~3–4 hrs)
- **Mumbai** (overnight), **Delhi** (overnight via Nizamuddin/NDLS routes)
- **Jaipur/Ajmer**, **Ahmedabad/Vadodara** sides
- **Nagpur/Itarsi**, and southern routes toward Hyderabad

Recent years also added **Mahakal-liveried trains** on key routes — appropriate transport for this yatra. Always check current schedules on IRCTC/NTES; the [12 Jyotirlingas guide](/blog/12-jyotirlingas-list-mahakaleshwar/) covers pairing Omkareshwar into the same rail journey.

**Station → temple quarter:** 2–3 km; autos are plentiful and inexpensive. On festival days the last stretch is pedestrian — pack light or use a luggage-capable auto.

## By Road: Driving & Buses

- **Indore → Ujjain**: ~55 km via NH-52/state highways, 1–1.5 hrs. Frequent buses (regular + deluxe) from Indore's Sarwate/other stands.
- **Bhopal → Ujjain**: ~180–190 km, 3.5–4.5 hrs by road (or the easier train).
- **Omkareshwar → Ujjain**: ~140 km, ~3–4 hrs — the classic Jyotirlinga pairing ([route guide](/blog/mahakaleshwar-to-omkareshwar/)).
- **Self-drive**: roads are decent on main corridors; parking near the temple is the real constraint — most driving pilgrims park at the temple-quarter edge and walk (or pick a stay that assists with parking — [ours does](/amenities/)).
