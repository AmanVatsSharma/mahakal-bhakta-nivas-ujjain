## Facilities That Matter From Week Two

On a two-night trip, everything is a perk. By the second week, a short list becomes the whole experience:

- **Hot water and an attached bathroom** — reliability matters every single morning, not just on day one
- **Pure veg food on site** — three meals a day, every day, is the difference between a stay and a logistics problem
- **24/7 power backup** — a month of grid fluctuations should never reach your room
- **Free WiFi** — hospital updates, work calls, booking darshan slots
- **Parking** — if you arrived by car or bike, it stays put and stays safe
- **A location you can live in** — 200 m from the temple gate means your daily walk IS the itinerary: darshan, Ram Ghat at 500 m, the bazaar, and home
