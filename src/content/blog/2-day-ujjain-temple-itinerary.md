Two days is the sweet spot for Ujjain: enough for Bhasma Aarti, unhurried darshan, the evening river ghats, and the city's other great temples — without the fatigue of a rushed day-trip. This is the itinerary our guests follow most often, tuned around real temple timings.

## Day 1 — Arrive, Darshan, Sandhya Aarti, Mahakal Lok

**Afternoon: check in near the temple**
Arrive by 2–3 PM and check into a room within walking distance of the temple quarter (this single decision makes the whole plan work — see [where to stay](/blog/where-to-stay-near-mahakaleshwar-temple/)). Freshen up, have an early pure-veg thali dinner by 5:30 PM.

**6:15 PM — Mahakaleshwar darshan + Sandhya Aarti**
Enter for darshan before the 7:00 PM Sandhya Aarti. The sanctum during evening aarti — lamps, conch, bells — is the trip's first unforgettable moment. Phones and leather stay at your room; carry only essentials.

**8:00 PM — Mahakal Lok corridor**
Walk the illuminated Shiva-sculpture corridor at night when the lighting is at its best. 45–60 minutes, plenty of photo spots. (Full details: [Mahakal Lok guide](/blog/mahakal-lok-corridor-guide/).)

**9:30 PM — Optional: Shayan Aarti (11 PM) or rest**
If you have a Bhasma Aarti booking tomorrow, sleep now. If not, the 11 PM Shayan Aarti is a serene bonus — the temple empties and the ritual feels intimate.

## Day 2 — Bhasma Aarti, Ghats & the Great Temples

**3:00 AM wake-up (Bhasma Aarti booked?)**
Walk to the temple with your booking printout + original ID. The 4:00–6:00 AM Bhasma Aarti is the reason many devotees make this journey — the [booking guide](/blog/how-to-book-bhasma-aarti-mahakaleshwar/) covers the process. Return by 6:30 AM for breakfast and a short rest.

*(No booking? Do regular darshan at 6:30 AM instead — the calmest window of the day.)*

**9:30 AM — Ram Ghat & Shipra**
Drive/walk to Ram Ghat on the Shipra river — the spiritual waterfront of Ujjain. Morning riverside aarti atmosphere, feeding the river, and a short boat ride in season.

**11:00 AM — Harsiddhi Shakti Peeth**
One of the most powerful Shakti Peeths, a short ride from the ghats. The twin deep stambhs lit during festivals are iconic.

**12:30 PM — Lunch + hotel break**
Return for lunch and check-out (or keep the room for day-use if departing late — ask your host; we accommodate luggage and rest whenever occupancy allows).

**3:00 PM — Chintaman Ganesh & Sandipani Ashram**
The ancient swayambhu Ganesh temple, then nearby Sandipani Ashram — where Krishna, Balram and Sudama are said to have studied, with the numbered stone steps of Gomti Kund.

**5:00 PM — Kal Bhairav**
Ujjain's guardian — the Bhairav temple where offerings of alcohol are traditionally made (a unique, intense experience; non-drinkers simply take darshan). Allow 60–90 minutes including queue on busy days.

**7:00 PM — Departure**
Trains and buses connect to Indore (~1–1.5 hrs), Bhopal (~3 hrs), and onward.
