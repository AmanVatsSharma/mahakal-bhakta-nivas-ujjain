# Jyotirlinga Circuit Guide from Ujjain: Planning a Multi-Temple Pilgrimage from Mahakaleshwar

Ujjain is not just one of the 12 Jyotirlingas — it is also the best starting point for a multi-temple pilgrimage. The city is well-connected by rail and road, sits in the heart of Madhya Pradesh, and offers the Mahakaleshwar experience that sets the spiritual tone for everything that follows.

If you are planning to visit more than one Jyotirlinga and want Ujjain to be your base, this guide maps out the best routes, durations, costs, and logistics.

## Why Ujjain Is the Ideal Starting Point for a Jyotirlinga Circuit

Ujjain's connectivity makes it the natural hub for Jyotirlinga pilgrimages:

**By rail:** Ujjain Junction connects directly to major cities — Indore (1 hour), Bhopal (4 hours), Kota (3.5 hours), Ahmedabad (8 hours). From any of these hubs, you can reach other Jyotirlingas.
