Ujjain is the city of Mahakal — and once every twelve years, it becomes the centre of the Hindu world. **Simhastha Kumbh Mela 2028** is approaching, and the scale is historic: government planning has indicated around **12 crore devotees** expected across the mela, backed by thousands of crores of rupees in infrastructure — new ghats along a developed Shipra riverfront, road widening, and expanded mela zones.

If attending is even a possibility for you, start planning your stay now. This guide explains what we know, what to expect, and exactly how to secure a room.

## What Simhastha Actually Is

Simhastha (Ujjain's Kumbh) is tied to Jupiter's transit into Leo, when millions gather to bathe in the Shipra river and take Mahakal darshan. Unlike Haridwar/Prayagraj/Nashik versions, Ujjain's mela is centred on a living temple city whose permanent infrastructure — the temple quarter, the ghats, the old city — stays exactly where it is. That's why **fixed rooms near the temple are the defining constraint** of Simhastha planning.

## What We Know So Far (2026 vantage)

- **The event window**: 2028, traditionally spanning roughly a month around April–May. **Exact Shahi Snan (royal bath) dates are announced officially** — do not plan around rumoured dates.
- **Scale**: ~12 crore expected footfall per government statements; compare with ~2.5–3 crore per day on peak snan days concentrated near the ghats.
- **Infrastructure**: a ~29-km Shipra riverfront development, new ghats, four-laning of key approach roads (Agar–Indore road works etc.), and mela-zone utilities — all already under execution.
- **Accommodation landscape**: mela tent colonies, government/camp facilities, homestays — and the small, fixed supply of temple-quarter buildings like ours.

## The Room Math Every Pilgrim Should Understand

The temple quarter's permanent room supply serves ordinary-day Ujjain comfortably. During Simhastha:

1. **Demand multiplies several-fold** on snan days specifically.
2. **Authorities may regulate** pricing and allotments as the event nears (as happens in every Kumbh city).
3. **Location premium peaks**: a room 400 m from the temple gate versus 4 km away is a different pilgrimage entirely at 3 AM on a snan day.

Practical consequences:

- Book **the day official dates are announced** — permanent rooms will commit fast.
- Prefer bookings with **flexible cancellation** until dates firm up.
- Consider **shoulder-window stays**: the mela's quiet first week often offers a profound experience with a fraction of peak-day crowds.

## If You Cannot Get a Temple-Quarter Room

- **Mela tent colonies** — the authentic Kumbh experience; book through official mela authority channels when they open.
- **Indore as base** (~55 km) — viable for shoulder dates, unrealistic for peak snan mornings.
- **Day-arrival strategy** — arrive pre-dawn by road/rail, bathe, darshan, depart by afternoon; exhausting but workable if you must.
