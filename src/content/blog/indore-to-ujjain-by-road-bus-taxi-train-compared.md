The Indore-to-Ujjain route is one of the busiest pilgrimage corridors in Madhya Pradesh. At just **55 km**, the journey can take as little as an hour by train or 1–1.5 hours by road. The right choice depends on your budget, group size, and how much time you have. Here is a full comparison of bus, taxi, and train options — with timings, routes, costs, and tips.

---

## Distance and Route Overview

Indore to Ujjain runs along **NH52**, a well-maintained four-lane national highway. The road is in excellent condition year-round and has clear signage. There is also a slower alternate route via **SH27** that passes through smaller towns — useful only if NH52 is congested during major festivals.

- **Distance:** 55 km by road, 57 km by rail
- **Drive time:** 1–1.5 hours (normal conditions)
- **Festival season:** Add 20–30 minutes during Sawan, Mahashivratri, and Simhastha

## Transport Options Compared

### Taxi — Most Convenient

Taxis offer door-to-door service and complete flexibility on timing. This is the most popular choice for families and small groups making a day trip or overnight visit to Mahakaleshwar.

**Where to book:**
- Ola Outstation and Uber Intercity (app-based, reliable)
- Local taxi stands at Indore Railway Station and Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport
- Pre-booked taxi operators near [Mahakal Bhakta Nivas](/rooms/) who also offer return pickup

**Estimated fares (one way):**

| Vehicle Type | Seating | Approx. Fare (INR) |
|---|---|---|
| Sedan (Dzire / Swift) | 4 | 800 – 1,200 |
| SUV (Ertiga / Xylo) | 6–7 | 1,200 – 1,800 |
| Luxury SUV | 6 | 1,800 – 2,500 |

**Round-trip fares** (Indore – Ujjain – Indore, with waiting time) typically range from ₹1,500–₹2,500 for a sedan. If you are staying overnight, negotiate the waiting charge upfront — most drivers charge ₹200–₹300 per hour after the first drop.

**Tips:**
- Book Ola/Uber Outstation the evening before — morning slots fill fast on Mondays and festival days
- Confirm the fare includes toll charges (NH52 has a small toll plaza near Ujjain)
- Ask the driver to wait or return at a fixed time if you are doing darshan

---

### Bus — Most Economical

Buses are the cheapest option and run frequently throughout the day. MP Roadways and private operators both serve the route.

**Departure points in Indore:**
- **Navlakha Bus Stand** — primary departure point for Ujjain-bound buses
- **Sarwate Bus Stand** — fewer departures but convenient if you are near the city center

**Arrival in Ujjain:**
- **Ujjain ISBT** — 2.5 km from Mahakaleshwar Temple; auto-rickshaws available (₹30–₹80)

**Schedule and fares:**

| Bus Type | Frequency | Fare (INR) | Journey Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary (Non-AC) | Every 30 min | 80 – 120 | 1.5 – 2 hours |
| Semi-Luxury (Non-AC) | Every 45 min | 120 – 180 | 1.5 hours |
| Volvo AC | Every 1 hour | 200 – 350 | 1 – 1.5 hours |

- **First bus:** around 5:30 AM
- **Last bus:** around 10:30 PM
- **Book online:** RedBus, AbhiBus, or MP Roadways portal

**Tips:**
- Volvo AC buses are worth the extra ₹100–₹150 for comfort in summer
- The last bus from Ujjain to Indore leaves around 10:00 PM — plan your evening aarti accordingly
- Avoid festival-day buses if you have a fixed schedule; delays of 1–2 hours are common

### Train — Fastest Option

The Indore–Ujjain rail corridor is short, frequent, and surprisingly underused by pilgrims who default to road travel. If speed is your priority, the train wins.

**Key trains:**

| Train | Departure (Indore) | Arrival (Ujjain) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 095 Indore–Ujjain Passenger | 6:15 AM | ~7:15 AM | ~1 hour |
| 229 Indore–Ujjain Intercity | 9:00 AM | ~10:10 AM | ~1 hour 10 min |
| 096 Ujjain–Indore Passenger | 5:45 PM | ~6:45 PM | ~1 hour |

- **Ujjain Junction to temple:** 3 km, 10 minutes by auto (₹30–₹80)
- **Booking:** IRCTC website or app; Tatkal opens one day before at 10:00 AM
- **Tip:** Book Tatkal if planning last-minute — seats usually available for this short route

**Best for:** Solo travelers, couples, and anyone staying near Ujjain Junction who wants the fastest option. Less ideal if you are carrying large luggage or traveling with elderly family members (auto transfer adds hassle).

### Self-Drive — Maximum Freedom

If you have your own car or motorcycle, NH52 is a pleasant drive. Fuel stations, dhabas, and rest stops appear every 10–15 km.

- **Parking near Mahakaleshwar Temple:** Designated lots charge ₹20–₹50 per day
- **Private parking:** Available near several dharamshalas — see [rooms near Mahakaleshwar](/rooms/) for options
- **Caution:** Parking gets extremely tight on Mondays, Pradosha days, and festival days. Arrive by 8:00 AM to guarantee a spot

## Historical Note: The Sacred Road

The Indore–Ujjain route follows a path that pilgrims have walked for over two millennia. Ujjain — ancient **Avantika** — was the capital of the legendary King Vikramaditya and one of the twelve Jyotirlinga sites where Lord Shiva appeared as Mahakaleshwar. The city finds mention in the Puranas, the Mahabharata, and Kalidasa's *Meghaduta*. Every pilgrim traveling this 55 km stretch today is walking the same road that devotees, scholars, and emperors have used since antiquity. For a deeper look at Ujjain's history, read our [Ujjain Heritage Walk guide](/blog/ujjain-heritage-walk-guide/).

## Best Time to Travel from Indore

- **Early morning (6:00–8:00 AM):** Cool, light traffic, easy darshan on arrival
- **Late evening (after 8:00 PM):** Avoid if returning same day — last buses/trains may not suit your schedule
- **Mondays:** Heaviest pilgrim traffic; leave Indore by 6:30 AM to beat the crowd
- **Festival days (Mahashivratri, Sawan Mondays, Simhastha):** Expect delays on all modes; book transport 2–3 days in advance

## Before You Go: Essentials Checklist

- Carry a **government-issued photo ID** — required for Bhasma Aarti booking and temple entry
- Wear **modest, traditional clothing** — avoid shorts and sleeveless tops for darshan
- Remove **leather items** (belts, wallets, watch straps) before entering the temple
- Carry **cash** — Ujjain's temple area and ghats are largely cash-only
- Keep a **charged phone** with offline maps downloaded — network can be spotty near the ghats

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which is the best mode of transport from Indore to Ujjain?

For families and groups, a **taxi** is the most convenient — door-to-door service, flexible timing, and comfortable for the full day. For solo travelers on a budget, the **bus** or **train** are excellent choices. The train is fastest at ~1 hour, while buses give you the most departure flexibility. The best choice depends on your group size, budget, and how much luggage you carry.

### Q: How much does a taxi from Indore to Ujjain cost?

A one-way taxi costs approximately **₹800–₹1,200** for a sedan (4-seater) and **₹1,200–₹1,800** for a 6–7 seater SUV. Round-trip fares with waiting time range from **₹1,500–₹2,500** for a sedan. Ola/Uber Outstation and local taxi operators both serve this route.

### Q: How far is Indore from Ujjain by road?

Indore is **55 km** from Ujjain via NH52. The drive takes **1–1.5 hours** under normal conditions. During peak pilgrimage seasons (Sawan, Mahashivratri), add 20–30 minutes. By train, the distance is 57 km and the journey is approximately **1 hour**.

### Q: Is a day trip from Indore to Ujjain worth it?

Yes — a day trip is very feasible if you leave Indore by 7:00 AM and return by 8:00 PM. You can comfortably cover Mahakaleshwar darshan, Mahakal Lok Corridor, and the evening Sandhya Aarti. However, a day trip does not leave time for the Bhasma Aarti, which requires a 3:30 AM arrival at the temple. For that experience, plan an overnight stay — see [rooms near Mahakaleshwar Temple](/rooms/) for options.

### Q: What is the last bus from Ujjain to Indore?

The last bus from Ujjain ISBT to Indore typically departs around **10:00–10:30 PM**. MP Roadways and private operators both run late-night services. If you are attending the 7:00 PM Sandhya Aarti, plan to board your return bus by 9:00 PM to avoid missing it. Always confirm the schedule on RedBus or the MP Roadways portal on the day of travel.

### Q: Are there direct trains from Indore to Ujjain?

Yes. The **Indore–Ujjain Passenger** and **Indore–Ujjain Intercity Express** run daily between the two cities. The journey takes approximately **1 hour**. Ujjain Junction is 3 km from Mahakaleshwar Temple — a 10-minute auto-rickshaw ride. Tatkal booking opens one day in advance on IRCTC and seats are usually available for this short route.

### Q: Do I need to book transport in advance for Mahashivratri?

Absolutely. During **Mahashivratri**, **Sawan Mondays**, and **Simhastha**, all modes of transport are in extremely high demand. Book your taxi 3–4 days in advance, reserve bus seats via RedBus at least 2 days ahead, and book train Tatkal as soon as it opens. If you are visiting during a festival, also book your accommodation early — rooms near the temple fill up weeks ahead. Check our [Mahashivratri guide](/blog/mahashivratri-mahakaleshwar-guide/) for festival-specific planning tips.

### Q: What should I carry for the Indore to Ujjain trip?

Carry a **government photo ID** (required for temple entry and Bhasma Aarti), **cash** (the temple area is largely cash-only), a **charged phone** with offline maps, and wear **modest traditional clothing**. Avoid leather items (belts, wallets, watch straps) — these must be removed before entering Mahakaleshwar Temple. Carry a small water bottle and prasad for the journey.
