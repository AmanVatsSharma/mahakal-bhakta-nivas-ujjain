A Mahakal yatra is one of India's most affordable pilgrimages — the temple itself asks nothing for darshan, the city eats you well for thali prices, and rooms near the sanctum start at ₹600. But budgets still leak in predictable places. Here is the honest math, line by line.

## The Big Picture (Per Person, Excluding Travel-to-Ujjain)

| Traveller | 1 Day | 2 Days / 1 Night |
|---|---|---|
| Solo pilgrim (dormitory) | ₹700–1,000 | ₹1,200–1,600 |
| Couple (non-AC double) | ₹1,300–1,800 | ₹2,400–3,300 |
| Family of 4 (AC/family room) | ₹2,200–3,000 | ₹4,500–6,500 |

Add your train/bus/fuel to reach Ujjain (e.g., Indore–Ujjain bus ≈ ₹100–200/person; overnight trains from metro cities vary widely).

## Line 1: The Room (₹600–₹2,500)

The biggest lever, covered in detail in our [budget rooms guide](/blog/rooms-near-mahakaleshwar-under-1500/):

- **Dormitory ₹600** — solo travellers, groups
- **Non-AC double ₹800–1,200** — couples; perfect Oct–Mar
- **AC double ₹1,200–1,500** — families & Apr–Jun visits
- **Family room ₹1,400–2,500** — 4–5 people ([see ours](/rooms/))

**Booking directly on WhatsApp** saves OTA fees — [2-minute process](/blog/online-room-booking-ujjain-whatsapp-guide/).

## Line 2: Food (₹150–300/person/day)

The temple quarter is a budget-eater's dream, all pure veg:

- Poha-jalebi breakfast: ₹30–60
- Thali lunch/dinner: ₹80–150
- Chai, prasad, snacks: ₹50–100/day
- Dharamshala in-house meals (like ours) sit in the same band — [see meals](/amenities/)

## Line 3: Temple & Rituals (₹0 upward)

- **General darshan: free**
- **Bhasma Aarti**: ~₹200 online (as of 2026; ₹100 offline counter) — [booking guide](/blog/how-to-book-bhasma-aarti-mahakaleshwar/)
- **Sheeghra Darshan / sevas**: portal-notified rates, optional ([seva guide](/blog/abhishek-pooja-booking-mahakaleshwar/))
- Prasad & offerings: budget ₹100–300 by devotion

## Line 4: Local Transport (₹100–400 total)

The temple circuit is compact — autos between temple quarter, Ram Ghat, Harsiddhi and Kal Bhairav cost little. Stay central and this line nearly vanishes ([why location saves money](/blog/where-to-stay-near-mahakaleshwar-temple/)).

## Line 5: The Leak-Points (Where Budgets Actually Break)

1. **Festival-date rooms** — Shravan/Mahashivratri weekends price and availability both spike; book early ([festival calendar](/blog/ujjain-festival-days-guide/))
2. **Auto circuits without negotiation** — fix the full-day circuit price upfront
3. **Prasad shop impulse** — the counters near the gate are for flowers; the sponsored-seva prasad comes via receipt
4. **Day-trip taxis waiting on meter-time** — a ₹900 day-room beats a ₹1,500 waiting cab ([day-room hack](/blog/one-day-ujjain-darshan-plan/))
5. **OTA booking fees** — book direct

## Two Real Sample Budgets

**The couple's honest weekend (from Indore):**
Bus ₹160×2 + non-AC double ₹1,000 + food ₹900 (2 days, couple) + autos ₹150 + aarti pass ₹200×1 + prasad ₹150 ≈ **₹2,720 for two**.

**The family of four's comfortable 2 days:**
Train ₹800 (4 people, short hop) + family room ₹1,800 + food ₹1,600 + autos/circuit ₹400 + seva ₹500 + prasad ₹300 ≈ **₹5,400**.

Both include everything except shopping — and Ujjain's shopping street is temptingly reasonable ([guide](/blog/food-shopping-ujjain-guide/)).

## Where to Spend, Where to Save

- **Spend**: room location (walking distance) — it compounds into every aarti and queue
- **Save**: room fanciness (you are barely in it), eat local thalis, book direct, travel weekdays
- **Never save on**: the 4 AM wake-up margin — proximity is safety and sanity ([book a close room](/rooms/))
