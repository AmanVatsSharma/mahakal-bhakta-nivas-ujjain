# Pune to Ujjain: Distance, Routes, and Best Way to Travel

Planning a pilgrimage to the holy city of Ujjain and the sacred Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga? If you are traveling from **Pune**, one of Maharashtra's largest cities, you have several options to reach Ujjain. This guide covers everything you need to know — distance, routes, modes of transport, costs, and useful tips — so you can focus on your spiritual journey with confidence.

---

## Distance Between Pune and Ujjain

The **road distance** from Pune to Ujjain is approximately **650 to 700 kilometers**, depending on the exact route taken. By air, the straight-line distance is around **560 kilometers**. The journey can take anywhere from **10 to 14 hours** by road and about **1 hour 30 minutes** by flight.

Ujjain, located on the banks of the **Kshipra River** in Madhya Pradesh, is one of the four sites where the legendary **Kumbh Mela** is held. The city has deep spiritual significance and draws millions of devotees every year to the **Mahakaleshwar Temple**, one of the 12 revered Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.

---

## Best Routes to Ujjain from Pune

### Route 1: Pune – Mumbai – Vadodara – Ujjain (via NH-48)

This is one of the most popular and well-maintained routes.

- **Pune to Mumbai:** ~150 km via Mumbai-Pune Expressway
- **Mumbai to Vadodara:** ~400 km via NH-48
- **Vadodara to Ujjain:** ~175 km via SH-27

**Total distance:** ~725 km  
**Approximate travel time:** 13–15 hours by car or bus

This route offers excellent road conditions and passes through major towns where you can stop for meals and fuel. It is ideal for families and groups traveling by private vehicle.

### Route 2: Pune – Aurangabad – Indore – Ujjain (via NH-53)

A slightly longer but equally scenic option.

- **Pune to Aurangabad:** ~230 km via NH-53
- **Aurangabad to Indore:** ~540 km via NH-52
- **Indore to Ujjain:** ~55 km via SH-27

**Total distance:** ~825 km  
**Approximate travel time:** 15–17 hours by car

This route is less congested and offers a comfortable drive, especially for those who wish to visit the **Ajanta and Ellora Caves** en route.

### Route 3: Pune – Solapur – Hyderabad – Ujjain (via NH-65)

For travelers coming from the southern parts of Pune, this route is worth considering.

- **Pune to Solapur:** ~250 km via NH-65
- **Solapur to Hyderabad:** ~410 km via NH-65
- **Hyderabad to Ujjain:** ~560 km via NH-52

**Total distance:** ~1,220 km  
**Approximate travel time:** 20–22 hours

This is a longer route and is best suited for those combining their trip with a visit to **Hyderabad** or other destinations in Telangana.

## Modes of Travel

### By Flight

The fastest way to reach Ujjain from Pune is by air.

- **Nearest airport to Ujjain:** **Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport**, Indore (IDR) — about 55 km from Ujjain
- **Airlines operating Pune-Indore:** IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara
- **Flight duration:** ~1 hour 15 minutes
- **One-way ticket cost:** INR 2,500 to INR 7,000 (varies by season and advance booking)
- **From Indore airport to Ujjain:** ~1 hour 15 minutes by taxi or bus (INR 800–1,500)

**Tip:** Book flights 2–3 weeks in advance for the best fares, especially during **Kumbh Mela** or **Maha Shivaratri** when demand surges.

### By Train

Several trains connect Pune and Ujjain, offering an economical and comfortable option.

- **Popular trains:** Pune-Ujjain Express, Indore-Pune Express
- **Travel time:** 16–20 hours (depending on the train and route)
- **Ticket prices:**
  - Sleeper Class (SL): INR 500–INR 800
  - 3rd AC (3A): INR 1,200–INR 1,800
  - 2nd AC (2A): INR 1,800–INR 2,800

**Tip:** Book your train tickets in advance on the IRCTC website or app. For a more comfortable overnight journey, opt for 3rd AC.

### By Bus

Multiple state-run and private bus services run between Pune and Ujjain daily.

- **Operators:** MSRTC, Shiv Sai Travels, VRL Travels, Patel Travels
- **Travel time:** 14–17 hours
- **Ticket prices:**
  - Non-AC Sleeper: INR 1,000–INR 1,400
  - AC Sleeper: INR 1,800–INR 2,500
  - Volvo AC: INR 2,000–INR 3,000

**Tip:** Overnight AC sleeper buses are a good balance of comfort and cost. Check reviews and operator reputation before booking.

### By Car or Bike

Driving from Pune to Ujjain is a memorable experience for those who enjoy road trips.

- **Fuel cost (approx.):** INR 4,500–INR 6,000 (petrol) or INR 3,000–INR 4,000 (diesel)
- **Toll charges (approx.):** INR 2,000–INR 3,000
- **Food and rest stops:** INR 1,000–INR 2,000

**Tip:** Plan rest stops at **Satara**, **Kolhapur**, or **Solapur** for meals. Drive safely on the ghat sections and carry water and snacks.

## Best Time to Visit Ujjain

The ideal time to visit Ujjain is between **October and March**, when the weather is cool and pleasant. Summer months (April–June) can be extremely hot, with temperatures crossing 45 degrees Celsius.

Key events to plan your visit around:

- **Maha Shivaratri (February/March):** The grandest festival at Mahakaleshwar Temple. Expect large crowds and special ceremonies.
- **Kumbh Mela (once every 12 years):** The next Kumbh Mela in Ujjain (Simhastha) will attract millions of pilgrims from around the world.
- **Navratri (September/October):** A vibrant and spiritually charged time in Ujjain.

## Things to Do in Ujjain

Beyond the Mahakaleshwar Temple, Ujjain has several other attractions:

- **Ram Ghat:** A scenic ghat on the Kshipra River, perfect for an evening stroll and boat ride.
- **Bada Ganesh Temple:** Known for its massive idol of Lord Ganesha, located near Mahakaleshwar Temple.
- **Kal Bhairav Temple:** Dedicated to Lord Bhairav, the guardian deity of Ujjain.
- **Harsiddhi Temple:** An ancient Durga temple with a unique idol and stunning architecture.
- **Vikram University:** A historic institution with a beautiful campus.

## Packing Tips for Your Journey

- Carry a **small bag** with essentials for the journey: water bottle, snacks, hand sanitizer, and any necessary medications.
- Dress modestly when visiting temples — avoid shorts and sleeveless tops.
- Bring **comfortable walking shoes**; Ujjain involves a fair amount of walking, especially around the temple complex.
- Carry a **power bank** for your phone, as you may be out the entire day.
- Keep a **photocopy of your ID** and important documents.

---

## Safety Tips

- Ujjain is generally a safe city for pilgrims, but always keep your belongings secure.
- Avoid traveling alone late at night, especially in isolated areas.
- Use only **registered taxis or auto-rickshaws** and insist on a meter or agree on a fare before starting the ride.
- Carry a **first-aid kit** and any personal medications, as chemist shops near the temple can be crowded.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How far is Ujjain from Pune by road?

The road distance from Pune to Ujjain is approximately **650 to 700 kilometers**, depending on the route. The journey takes about **13 to 15 hours** by car or bus.

### 2. Is there a direct flight from Pune to Ujjain?

There is no direct flight to Ujjain. The nearest airport is **Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport in Indore**, about 55 km from Ujjain. You can take a direct flight from Pune to Indore and then a taxi or bus to Ujjain.

### 3. What is the cheapest way to travel from Pune to Ujjain?

The most economical option is by **train**. Sleeper class tickets cost between **INR 500 and INR 800**. State-run buses are also a budget-friendly option, starting from around **INR 1,000**.

### 4. How many days should I spend in Ujjain?

A **2 to 3-day trip** is ideal. Day 1 can be dedicated to Mahakaleshwar Temple and nearby temples. Day 2 can cover other ghats and temples. If you are attending a festival like Maha Shivaratri, consider extending your stay.

### 5. Are there vegetarian food options near the temple?

Yes, Ujjain is famous for its **pure vegetarian food**. You will find numerous restaurants, thali joints, and street food stalls near the temple and ghats. Popular local dishes include **poha**, **dal bafla**, and **bhutte ka kees**.

### 6. Can I visit Ujjain as a solo female traveler?

Ujjain is generally safe for solo female travelers, especially during the day. It is advisable to stay in a centrally located, well-reviewed hotel or guest house. Avoid isolated areas after dark and use official or pre-booked taxis.

### 7. Is the Mahakaleshwar Temple open on all days?

Yes, the Mahakaleshwar Temple is open **every day**. However, during festivals and auspicious days, expect larger crowds. Check the temple's official timings before your visit.

### 8. What are the temple darshan timings?

The Mahakaleshwar Temple opens at **4:00 AM** and closes at **11:00 PM**. There is a brief closing between **3:30 PM and 4:00 PM** for cleaning. **Bhasma Aarti**, the famous ash ritual, is performed early in the morning and requires prior booking or special passes.
