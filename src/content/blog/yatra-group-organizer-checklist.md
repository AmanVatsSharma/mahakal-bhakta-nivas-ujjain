# Yatra Group Organizer Checklist: Planning a Group Trip to Mahakaleshwar Ujjain

Leading a yatra group to Mahakaleshwar Temple is one of the most rewarding responsibilities a pilgrimage organizer can take on. But it is also one of the most demanding. A group of 20–50 devotees relies on you for accommodation, transport, darshan coordination, meals, and safety — all while they are focused on their spiritual experience.

This checklist covers everything you need to plan, execute, and wrap up a successful group trip to Mahakaleshwar Ujjain. Follow it step by step and your group will have a smooth, memorable yatra.
