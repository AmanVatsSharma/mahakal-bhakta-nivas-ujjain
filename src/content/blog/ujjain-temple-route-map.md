# Ujjain Temple Route Map: Complete Guide to Covering All Major Temples in One Day

Ujjain is one of the seven holiest cities in Hinduism (Mokshapuri), home to over 100 temples spread across the city. If you have just one day in Ujjain, planning the right route order can save you hours of backtracking and ensure you cover all the important temples comfortably.

This guide provides a complete **Ujjain temple route map** with distances, suggested visit order, timings for each temple, transport options, and a suggested daily schedule.

## Major Temples in Ujjain

These are the 10 most important temples that pilgrims and tourists visit:

1. **Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga** — The main temple, one of the 12 Jyotirlingas
2. **Harsiddhi Temple** — Dedicated to Goddess Harsiddhi, one of the 51 Shakti Peethas
3. **Gopal Mandir** — Built by Maharani Ahilyabai Holkar, dedicated to Lord Krishna
4. **Chintaman Ganesh Temple** — Dedicated to Lord Ganesha, the temple where he removes worries
5. **Kal Bhairav Temple** — Dedicated to Lord Bhairav, the guardian deity of Ujjain
6. **Ram Ghat** — Sacred ghat on the Shipra River
7. **Mangalnath Temple** — Dedicated to Lord Shiva as Mangalnath (Mars deity)
8. **Sandipani Ashram** — The ashram of Guru Sandipani where Lord Krishna studied
9. **Bhartrihari Cave** — Ancient cave associated with poet-king Bhartrihari
10. **Vikram Kirti Mandir** — Museum and cultural center near Mahakaleshwar

## Temple Route Map with Distances

Here is the suggested order that minimizes backtracking, with approximate distances:

### Stop 1: Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga (Start Point)
- **Distance from Ujjain Junction:** 3 km
- **Darshan time:** 45–90 minutes
- **Best time to visit:** 4–7 AM (shortest queues)
- **Must-see:** Main Jyotirlinga, Bhasma Aarti (4 AM), Bade Ganesh temple adjacent

### Stop 2: Harsiddhi Temple
- **Distance from Mahakaleshwar:** 1.5 km
- **Travel time:** 5 minutes by auto
- **Darshan time:** 15–20 minutes
- **Best time:** 8–10 AM

### Stop 3: Gopal Mandir
- **Distance from Harsiddhi:** 1 km
- **Travel time:** 3 minutes by auto / 10 min walk
- **Darshan time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Best time:** 10–11 AM
- **Note:** Built by Ahilyabai Holkar with marble from the Somnath temple ruins

### Stop 4: Chintaman Ganesh Temple
- **Distance from Gopal Mandir:** 1 km
- **Travel time:** 3 minutes
- **Darshan time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Best time:** 11 AM–12 PM

### Stop 5: Kal Bhairav Temple
- **Distance from Chintaman Ganesh:** 2 km
- **Travel time:** 5 minutes
- **Darshan time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Best time:** 12–1 PM
- **Note:** Known for the unique offering of liquor to the deity

### Stop 6: Ram Ghat
- **Distance from Kal Bhairav:** 1.5 km
- **Travel time:** 5 minutes
- **Time to spend:** 20–30 minutes
- **Best time:** 1–3 PM (afternoon, avoid mid-day sun)
- **Note:** Sacred bathing ghat on the Shipra River — perfect for a peaceful break

### Stop 7: Mangalnath Temple
- **Distance from Ram Ghat:** 1 km
- **Travel time:** 3 minutes
- **Darshan time:** 10–15 minutes
- **Best time:** 3–4 PM

### Stop 8: Sandipani Ashram
- **Distance from Mangalnath:** 2 km
- **Travel time:** 5 minutes
- **Time to spend:** 15–20 minutes
- **Best time:** 4–5 PM

### Optional Stop 9: Bhartrihari Cave
- **Distance from Sandipani Ashram:** 1 km
- **Time to spend:** 15 minutes
- **Note:** Ancient cave, worth a quick visit if you have time

## Transport Options for the Temple Circuit

### Auto-Rickshaw (Best Option)
- **Full day hire:** ₹400–₹600 for the entire circuit
- **Per trip:** ₹50–₹100 per temple visit
- **Pros:** Flexible, door-to-door, shared options available
- **Cons:** Negotiate rates upfront to avoid overcharging

### Self-Drive / Rental Car
- **Daily rental:** ₹1,500–₹3,000
- **Fuel:** ~₹200 for the full circuit
- **Pros:** Maximum flexibility, no waiting
- **Cons:** Parking can be challenging near some temples

### Local Bus
- **Cost:** ₹10–₹20 per trip
- **Frequency:** Every 20–30 minutes
- **Pros:** Cheapest option
- **Cons:** Slower, less convenient with temple schedules

### Walking
- **Distance:** Total walking distance is approximately 8–10 km
- **Feasible only if:** You have plenty of time and are staying near Mahakaleshwar
- **Not recommended** if you have limited time

## Pro Tips for the Temple Circuit

1. **Start at 4 AM** for the Bhasma Aarti — it's the most spiritually significant event of the day and the queues are manageable.
2. **Carry water and snacks** — there are few food options along the route.
3. **Wear comfortable shoes** — you'll be walking and standing at temples throughout the day.
4. **Dress modestly** — carry a scarf for temples that require covered shoulders.
5. **Book accommodation near Mahakaleshwar** — saves travel time to and from the main temple.
6. **Visit during weekday mornings** — Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday mornings are the least crowded.

## FAQs

**Q1: Can I cover all temples in half a day?**
You can visit 4–5 temples in half a day. For the full circuit of 8–10 temples, plan a full day starting at 4 AM.

**Q2: Are all temples open every day?**
Yes, all major temples in Ujjain are open daily. Some have specific closing times (usually between 12 PM–3 PM for rest).

**Q3: Is photography allowed at all temples?**
Photography is restricted at Mahakaleshwar's garbhagriha. Most other temples allow photography in the courtyard. Always check with temple staff.

**Q4: What is the total cost for the temple circuit including transport?**
Approximately ₹400–₹600 for auto transport + ₹200–₹500 for darshan passes + ₹300–₹500 for food and prasad = ₹900–₹1,600 total per person.

**Q5: Are there guided temple tours available?**
Yes, local tour operators offer guided temple circuits. Contact the Ujjain Tourism office or ask at your hotel.

**Q6: Can I visit temples during the afternoon?**
Yes, but some temples close between 12 PM and 3 PM for cleaning/rest. Check timings in advance.

**Q7: What is the dress code for temple visits?**
Modest clothing required — no shorts, sleeveless tops, or skirts above the knee. See our [dress code guide](/blog/mahakaleshwar-rules-dress-code-locker.md).

**Q8: Are there eating options along the temple route?**
Yes, small food stalls are available near each temple. For sit-down meals, restaurants near the main market area offer thali meals for ₹150–₹300.
