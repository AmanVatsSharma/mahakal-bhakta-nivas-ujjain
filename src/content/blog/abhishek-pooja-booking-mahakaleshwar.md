Beyond darshan and aarti, Mahakaleshwar offers devotees the chance to personally perform **abhishek** — the ritual bathing of the Shiva linga — and sponsor specific pujas. For many families this is the emotional core of the yatra: a Mundan-and-abhishek for a child's first birthday, a Rudrabhishek for a parent's health, a simple milk abhishek as thanks. Here is how the booking actually works.

## What You Can Book

The [official portal](https://www.shrimahakaleshwar.mp.gov.in/) lists the temple's seva menu. Commonly available options include:

| Seva (indicative) | What it involves |
|---|---|
| **Rudrabhishek** | Full ritual bathing of the linga with recitation of Rudra sukta — the most popular sponsored seva |
| **Panchamrit Abhishek** | Bathing with the five sacred offerings (milk, curd, ghee, honey, sugar) |
| **Mahapooja / Mahabhog** | Grand worship and food offering in your family's name |
| **Bhasma/Sandhya/Shayan Aarti passes** | Attendance at the aartis |

**Fees change over time** — the portal shows live prices. Any "agent" quoting multiples of the portal rate is marking you up; the temple does not outsource bookings.

## Booking Step by Step

1. **Open the official portal** and register with your mobile number.
2. **Choose the seva** → select date → select time slot if applicable.
3. **Enter devotee details exactly as per photo ID** (name spelling matters at verification).
4. **Pay online** and save the receipt/QR confirmation.
5. **On the day:** carry original ID + printed booking, reach 30–45 minutes before the slot, and follow seva-desk signage.

## Documents & Rules

- Original government photo ID for every named devotee.
- Traditional attire; leather items and phones stay outside.
- Prasadam from sponsored pujas is generally collected at a designated counter after the ritual.
- Photography inside the sanctum area is prohibited even for seva participants.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Booking via middlemen** — "package agents" outside the temple or on social media resell portal sevas with fat commissions. The portal is open to everyone.
2. **Name mismatches** — booking with a nickname while carrying an ID with the formal name causes entry refusals. Use the ID spelling.
3. **Ignoring slot timing** — seva slots around Bhasma Aarti mean 3 AM arrival; evening sevas after 8 PM suit families better.
4. **Assuming same-day availability** — fine off-season; impossible in Shravan, on Mondays, and around festivals.

## With Children or Elderly Devotees?

- Morning Rudrabhishek means very early rising — an evening seva or Sandhya Aarti pass is often kinder.
- The temple facilitates senior citizens and differently-abled devotees with separate access; ask the seva desk on arrival.
- For infants' rituals (mundan etc.), barbers cluster near the temple gate — our front desk can point you to the family-preferred ones.
