Bhasma Aarti is the most sacred — and most sought-after — ritual at Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga in Ujjain. Every morning, Lord Mahakal is offered sacred ash (bhasma) in a ritual that exists nowhere else among the twelve Jyotirlingas. Devotees plan their entire Ujjain trip around attending it.

The catch: demand massively exceeds supply. Online slots for Bhasma Aarti have been booking out **months in advance** — as of early 2026, slots were reported full till May, with June bookings opening in mid-February. If attending Bhasma Aarti is your goal, this guide tells you exactly how the system works and what to do when slots are gone.

## What Is Bhasma Aarti and Why Is It Special?

Held daily from approximately **4:00 AM to 6:00 AM**, Bhasma Aarti is the first ritual of the day at Mahakaleshwar. The Shiva linga is bathed, decorated, and then offered sacred ash prepared from burnt dried cow dung. The tradition symbolises that even the Lord of Time is covered in ash — a reminder of impermanence.

Because the sanctum has limited capacity, only a small number of devotees can attend in person, which is exactly why a structured booking system exists.

## How Online Booking Works — Step by Step

1. **Go to the official portal**: [shrimahakaleshwar.mp.gov.in](https://www.shrimahakaleshwar.mp.gov.in/) — the only authorised channel. Beware of fake lookalike sites and agents charging heavy commissions.
2. **Create an account** with your mobile number and email.
3. **Select "Bhasma Aarti Booking"** and choose your date from available slots.
4. **Add devotee details** — full name exactly as on the government photo ID you will carry (Aadhaar, passport, DL, or voter ID). One ID per devotee.
5. **Pay online** (around ₹200 per person as of 2026) and download/print the booking confirmation.
6. **Carry the original ID** on the day. Entry is refused if the name on the pass does not match the ID.

**Helpline:** 1800-233-1008 (official temple helpline) for booking problems.

## When Do Slots Open?

The temple typically releases online slots **once a month for the whole following month** — for example, June 2026 bookings opened on 15 February. These disappear within minutes. Practical tips:

- Note the announcement date from the portal's notice section and be logged in beforehand.
- Book for flexible dates — Monday (Somvar) and Mahashivratri are the hardest to get.
- If travelling in a group, try booking 2–3 devotees at a time rather than the whole group in one attempt.

## If Online Slots Are Full — Your Plan B

1. **Offline counter booking**: the temple operates counters (including a dedicated facility for this) from the early hours. Arrive by 8–10 PM the previous night or very early morning; a limited number of passes are issued daily on first-come basis at around ₹100 per person (as of 2026).
2. **Adjust your date**: weekday attendance is far easier than weekends and Mondays.
3. **Attend the Sandhya or Shayan Aarti instead**: the 7:00 PM evening aarti and 11:00 PM night aarti need no hard booking in most periods and are deeply moving experiences.
4. **Ask your dharamshala for help**: hosts near the temple deal with the booking system daily. At Mahakal Bhakta Nivas we routinely help guests with aarti passes, early wake-up calls and temple guidance — just [message us on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/919989898989) when you book your room.

## Rules to Remember on Aarti Day

- Traditional/conservative clothing; avoid shorts, sleeveless tops and leather items (belt, wallet, purse) — deposit leather outside.
- Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside the sanctum area.
- Women and men are seated in separate sections for the aarti.
- Carry your booking printout **and original ID** — both are checked.
