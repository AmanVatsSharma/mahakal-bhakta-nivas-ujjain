# How to Book Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar: Step-by-Step Online Process & Pass Tips

The **Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar Temple** is one of the most spectacular and spiritually significant rituals in Hinduism. Every morning at 4 AM, the priests of Mahakaleshwar bathe the Jyotirlinga with sacred ash (bhasma) collected from homa fires — a ritual that has been performed continuously for centuries and is unique to this temple.

Because of its popularity, Bhasma Aarti requires advance booking. Passes are limited, demand is high, and the process can be confusing for first-time visitors.

This guide walks you through the complete online booking process — step by step — with tips for securing your pass, what documents you need, and what to do if your booking fails.

## Understanding Bhasma Aarti Passes

### What Is a Bhasma Aarti Pass?

A Bhasma Aarti pass grants you entry to the special darshan area for the 4 AM aarti. It is different from regular darshan — pass holders enter through a separate queue and are seated in a designated area inside the temple.

### Pass Categories

The temple typically offers several pass categories. Check the official booking portal for the current structure, but the general categories are:

**Standard Pass:**
- Entry to the Bhasma Aarti area
- Seating in the general pass-holder section
- Prasadam distribution after the aarti
- **Typical cost:** ₹100–₹500 per person (varies by season)

**VIP/Premium Pass (if available):**
- Closer seating to the Jyotirlinga
- Faster queue entry
- Additional prasadam or offerings
- **Typical cost:** ₹500–₹2,000 per person

**Group Pass:**
- Available for organized groups of 20+
- Booked through the temple administration office (not the online portal)
- Requires advance coordination
- **Cost:** Varies by group size

### How Many Passes Are Available?

The temple allocates a fixed number of Bhasma Aarti passes per day. The exact number varies but is typically in the range of 200–400 passes per day. On normal days, they may be available a week in advance. During Shivaratri and festivals, they sell out within hours of the booking window opening.

## Step-by-Step Bhasma Aarti Booking Process

### Step 1: Visit the Official Booking Portal

The Bhasma Aarti pass booking is handled through the official Ujjain temple booking system. The current portal URL is published on the [Ujjain district administration website](https://ujjain.nic.in) and the Mahakaleshwar Temple's official pages.

**Bookmark the portal** — you will need to check it regularly as booking windows open.

### Step 2: Create an Account / Log In

If you are a first-time user, you will need to:
1. Register with your name, phone number, and email
2. Verify your phone number via OTP
3. Create a password

If you have an existing account, simply log in.

**Tip:** Complete your registration a few days before the booking window opens. This saves time when passes become available.

### Step 3: Select "Bhasma Aarti Pass"

On the portal's homepage, look for the "Bhasma Aarti Pass" booking option. Select your desired date.

**Important:** Choose your date carefully. The booking portal shows availability for each date. Popular dates (Shivaratri, Mondays, festival days) will show "sold out" within hours.

### Step 4: Enter Pilgrim Details

For each pass, you need to provide:
- Full name (as it appears on your ID)
- Age
- Gender
- Government-issued ID type (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID)
- ID number

**Important:** The name on the pass must exactly match the name on your ID. Mismatched names result in denied entry at the gate.

### Step 5: Select Pass Quantity

You can typically book 2–4 passes per transaction. If your group is larger, make multiple transactions or request group coordination through the temple administration.

**Tip:** Book for all members of your group at the same time. Passes from different bookings may have different queue numbers, which means your group will not enter together.

### Step 6: Make Payment

The portal will show the total amount (number of passes x pass price). Payment is typically via:
- Debit/Credit card
- Net banking
- UPI

**Tip:** Have your payment method ready before the booking window opens. If you are slow to complete payment, your passes may be released to someone else in the queue.

### Step 7: Download/Print Your Pass

After successful payment, you will receive:
- A booking confirmation on the portal
- An SMS confirmation to your registered phone
- An email confirmation (check your spam folder if you don't see it)

**Download or screenshot the pass immediately.** You need it at the entry gate.

### Step 8: Entry Procedure on the Day

On the day of your Bhasma Aarti:
1. Arrive at Mahakaleshwar Temple by **3:00 AM at the latest** (queue starts at 2:30 AM)
2. Go to the "Bhasma Aarti Pass Holders" queue — not the general darshan queue
3. Show your pass (digital or printed) and your original photo ID
4. The name on your ID must match the name on the pass
5. Enter through the designated gate
6. Proceed to your seating area

**Important:** Do not lose your pass. If you lose it, entry may be denied. If you lose your ID, you may not be able to verify your identity.

   - Available on the booking portal after payment
   - Screenshot or printout is acceptable

3. **Booking confirmation SMS/email** — as backup if the portal has a technical issue
4. **A printed copy of the pass** — digital passes sometimes fail due to poor signal inside the temple

## Tips for Securing Your Bhasma Aarti Pass

### Book as Early as Possible

The booking window typically opens 7–15 days before the desired date. For Shivaratri, the window may open 30 days in advance.

**Set a reminder** for the booking opening date. Log in to the portal 10–15 minutes before the window opens so you are ready the moment it goes live.

### Use Multiple Devices

During high-demand periods, the portal can be slow or crash. Have two people booking simultaneously on different devices. If one connection fails, the other might succeed.

### Have Payment Ready

Do not wait until you see the booking screen to find your wallet or card. Have your payment method pre-loaded and ready. Speed matters.

### Book for Weekdays

If your schedule is flexible, book for a weekday (Tuesday–Thursday). Demand is lower than on Mondays (Shivaratri) and weekends, and passes are easier to get.

### Avoid Festival Dates Unless Specifically Planning for Them

Shivaratri, Ashadh month, and Samvat have massively inflated demand. Unless your trip is specifically for that festival, choose a regular date and you will get passes easily.

## What to Expect Inside the Bhasma Aarti

Once you enter the Bhasma Aarti area:

1. **Seating:** You will be directed to a designated seating area. VIP pass holders get closer seating; standard pass holders sit further back. The view of the Jyotirlinga is good from both sections.

2. **The Aarti:** The ritual begins with Vedic chanting. The priests perform the abhisheka (bathing) of the Jyotirlinga with bhasma (sacred ash) instead of water. This is followed by the aarti with lamps and the distribution of prasadam.

3. **Duration:** The Bhasma Aarti lasts 30–45 minutes. Do not leave early — the aarti has a spiritual arc and the most powerful moments are in the middle.

4. **Photography:** Strictly prohibited. Put your phone away and experience the aarti fully.

5. **After the aarti:** Collect prasadam (usually sugar crystals or peda) from the priests. Exit through the designated exit and join the general darshan queue if you want to see the Jyotirlinga up close.

## Troubleshooting: What to Do If Your Booking Fails

### The Portal Crashed

If the booking portal crashes during your transaction:
- Wait 10–15 minutes and retry
- Use a different browser or device
- Check if your payment went through (your bank statement will show)
- If you paid but did not receive a pass, contact the portal's customer support with your transaction ID

### Passes Are "Sold Out" for Your Date

If the date you want shows sold out:
- Check back daily — sometimes passes are released when other bookings are cancelled
- Try a different date (the day before or after)
- Contact the temple administration office directly — they sometimes have emergency passes for genuine cases

### Your Name Does Not Match Your ID

If you made a typo in your name during booking:
- Contact the temple administration immediately
- Provide your correct name and ID details
- Request a name correction (this is sometimes possible before the aarti date)

### You Lost Your Pass

If you lose your pass before the aarti:
- Log in to the booking portal and download a fresh copy
- Carry the digital copy on your phone AND a printout
- If you cannot access the portal, go to the temple administration office with your ID and transaction details

## FAQs

**Q1: How much does a Bhasma Aarti pass cost?**
Standard passes cost ₹100–₹500 per person. VIP passes (if available) cost ₹500–₹2,000. Group passes have variable pricing.

**Q2: How many Bhasma Aarti passes can I book at once?**
Typically 2–4 passes per transaction. For larger groups, make multiple bookings or contact the temple administration for group passes.

**Q3: When does the Bhasma Aarti booking portal open?**
The booking window usually opens 7–15 days before the desired date. For Shivaratri, it may open 30 days in advance. Check the official Ujjain portal for exact dates.

**Q4: What if I don't get a Bhasma Aarti pass?**
You can still visit Mahakaleshwar Temple for regular darshan. The morning, noon, and evening aartis are open to all without passes. The Sandhya Aarti at sunset is spectacular and accessible to everyone.

**Q5: Do children need Bhasma Aarti passes?**
Yes. Every person entering the Bhasma Aarti area needs a pass, including children. Infants in arms may be exempt — check with the temple office.

**Q6: Can I get a Bhasma Aarti pass on the day itself?**
No. Bhasma Aarti passes are not sold at the gate. They must be pre-booked online. Arriving at the gate without a pass means you will have to join the general darshan queue.
