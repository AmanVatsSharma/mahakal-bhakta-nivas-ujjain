# How to Book Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar: Step-by-Step Online Process & Pass Tips

The **Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar Temple** is one of the most spectacular and spiritually significant rituals in Hinduism. Every morning at 4 AM, the priests of Mahakaleshwar bathe the Jyotirlinga with sacred ash (bhasma) collected from homa fires — a ritual that has been performed continuously for centuries and is unique to this temple.

Because of its popularity, Bhasma Aarti requires advance booking. Passes are limited, demand is high, and the process can be confusing for first-time visitors.

This guide walks you through the complete online booking process — step by step — with tips for securing your pass, what documents you need, and what to do if your booking fails.

## Understanding Bhasma Aarti Passes

### What Is Bhasma Aarti?

Bhasma Aarti is the most sacred ritual at Mahakaleshwar Temple. Every morning before dawn, the priests prepare sacred ash (bhasma) from homa (fire) offerings and use it to bathe the Jyotirlinga. The ritual includes powerful Vedic mantras, the ringing of bells, and the lighting of hundreds of oil lamps. The atmosphere at 4 AM in the temple — dark, fragrant with incense, echoing with mantras — is something that photographs cannot capture.

### Why Booking Is Required

The Bhasma Aarti has been free for centuries, but the number of devotees who want to attend has grown enormously. To manage crowds, maintain security, and ensure a peaceful experience, the temple administration now requires advance booking. Passes are limited to a specific number each day, and they often sell out days or weeks in advance — especially during festivals like Mahashivratri and Sawan.

### Pass Types Available

| Pass Type | Who It's For | What's Included | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General Pass** | Individual devotees | Entry to the Bhasma Aarti hall | Free (but booking required) |
| **VIP Pass** | Senior citizens, disabled visitors | Priority entry, closer seating | ₹51–₹101 (dakshina) |
| **Special Darshan Pass** | Families, groups | Group entry, dedicated queue | ₹251–₹501 |
| **Aarti Participation Pass** | Devotees who want to participate | Includes puja items, closer access | ₹501–₹1,001 |

Note: Pass prices are subject to change. Verify current pricing on the official booking portal.

## Step-by-Step Booking Process

### Step 1: Visit the Official Booking Portal

The official Mahakaleshwar Bhasma Aarti booking is done through the **Ujjain Simhastha / Temple Administration portal**. The current official website for bookings is:

**Primary Portal:** Visit the official Ujjain temple website (search for "Mahakaleshwar Bhasma Aarti booking official")

**Alternative:** Many devotees use the **MP Tourism** portal (mptourism.com) which redirects to the temple booking system.

### Step 2: Register or Log In

If this is your first time:
1. Click on the **"Register"** or **"Sign Up"** button
2. Enter your mobile number — you will receive an OTP
3. Create a password and complete your profile with name and email
4. Verify your email if prompted

If you have already registered, simply log in with your mobile number and OTP.

### Step 3: Select Bhasma Aarti

1. On the dashboard, look for **"Bhasma Aarti Booking"** or **"Bhasmarti Pass"**
2. Select the date you want to attend — the calendar shows available dates
3. Select the number of passes needed (max 5 per booking for general passes)

### Step 4: Enter Devotee Details

For each pass, enter:
- **Full name** (as per your ID)
- **Age**
- **Gender**
- **ID proof type** (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License)
- **ID number**

**Important:** The name on the pass must match the name on your ID. You will need to show this ID at the temple gate.

### Step 5: Select Pass Type

Choose from the available pass types based on your needs:
- **General Pass:** Best for healthy adults who don't mind standing in the queue
- **VIP Pass:** Recommended for senior citizens (60+), pregnant women, and people with mobility issues
- **Special Darshan Pass:** Good for families with children who want to stay together

### Step 6: Make the Payment

If you have selected a paid pass type:
1. You will be redirected to the payment gateway
2. Payment options include debit card, credit card, UPI, and net banking
3. After successful payment, you will receive a **payment confirmation**
4. Save the transaction ID for reference

### Step 7: Download and Save Your Pass

After successful booking:
1. You will see a **booking confirmation** on the screen
2. A PDF pass will be generated — **download it immediately**
3. The pass will also be sent to your registered email and mobile number
4. **Take a screenshot** of the pass and keep it handy

**Important:** You must present the pass (digital or printed) at the temple entry gate. Without it, you will not be allowed to enter for Bhasma Aarti.

## What to Expect on the Day

### Entry Time
- **Doors open:** 3:30 AM (for Bhasma Aarti at 4 AM)
- **Latest entry:** 4:00 AM (strictly enforced)
- **Late arrivals** will not be admitted

### Entry Process
1. Arrive at the **North Gate** of Mahakaleshwar Temple by 3:30 AM
2. Show your **booking pass** (digital or printed) and **ID proof**
3. Go through security check (no phones, bags beyond certain size)
4. Deposit bags and phones at the cloak room (see our [mobile phone guide](/blog/mahakaleshwar-mobile-phone-rules/))
5. Join the queue for your pass category (VIP queue, General queue, etc.)
6. Enter the temple complex for the aarti

### During the Aarti
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes
- **Seating:** General pass holders stand; VIP pass holders get seated areas
- **Photography:** Strictly prohibited
- **Phones:** Must be deposited at cloak room
- **What to bring:** Nothing — just yourself and your faith

## Tips for Successful Booking

### Book Early
- **Weekdays:** Book 3–5 days in advance
- **Weekends:** Book 7–10 days in advance
- **Festivals:** Book 15–30 days in advance
- **Best booking time:** The portal opens at 10 AM every day — book as soon as it opens

### Booking Hacks
1. **Use multiple devices** — have family members try booking simultaneously on different phones
2. **Keep details ready** — have all devotee names and ID numbers typed out before starting
3. **Book for weekdays** — Tuesday to Thursday have more availability and smaller crowds
4. **Consider off-season** — avoid Sawan, Mahashivratri, and Amavasya dates if possible
5. **Refresh the portal** — if a date shows as full, keep refreshing — sometimes passes get released

### Common Booking Issues and Solutions

**Issue:** "No passes available for selected date"
- **Solution:** Try a different date, or keep checking throughout the day as cancellations may free up slots

**Issue:** "Payment failed but money was debited"
- **Solution:** Do NOT book again. The amount will be auto-refunded within 5–7 working days. Check the portal for your booking status.

**Issue:** "Pass not received on email/SMS"
- **Solution:** Log in to the portal with your credentials — the pass will be available in your booking history. Download it from there.

**Issue:** "Forgot to print pass"
- **Solution:** Digital copies on phone are accepted. Make sure your phone is charged and the pass screenshot is accessible.

## FAQ: Common Questions About Bhasma Aarti Booking

**Q: Can I get Bhasma Aarti passes without advance booking?**
A: No. Advance booking is mandatory for all Bhasma Aarti passes. There is no on-the-spot entry.

**Q: How many days in advance should I book?**
A: For weekdays, 3–5 days is usually enough. For weekends and festivals, book 2–3 weeks ahead.

**Q: Is Bhasma Aarti free?**
A: The aarti itself is free. However, certain pass types (VIP, Special Darshan) have a small dakshina charge of ₹51–₹1,001.

**Q: Can children attend Bhasma Aarti?**
A: Yes, children of all ages can attend. Children under 5 do not need a separate pass — they can accompany an adult with a valid pass.

**Q: What if I miss my entry time?**
A: The entry window is strict. If you miss the 4:00 AM cutoff, you will not be allowed to enter. Plan to arrive at least 30 minutes early.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I can't attend?**
A: Yes, cancellations are allowed up to 24 hours before the aarti. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 days.

**Q: Are there any restrictions for foreign tourists?**
A: Foreign tourists can book passes using their passport as ID. They are welcome at Bhasma Aarti. The experience is open to all faiths.

**Q: What is the dress code for Bhasma Aarti?**
A: Modest, full-coverage clothing. Men should wear dhoti or full trousers. Women should wear saree, salwar kameez, or long skirt with dupatta.
